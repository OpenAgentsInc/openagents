# Sources of Aethyr's sounds

Every sound Aethyr plays in a realm is listed here with its source and licence.
Add a line when you add a sound.

## Everglade's effects (GitHub #12832)

Everglade plays these by default. Each of its 47 sounds has 3 to 5 candidates in
[`apps/openagents-web/realms/everglade/sfx/`](../../apps/openagents-web/realms/everglade/sfx/),
named `NAME-CAND.ogg`, and 1 to 4 of them are its picks, the variants the page plays
([`everglade-bank.coax`](everglade-bank.coax) `SFXB_PICK`; their measures, for Audio Lab, are
in [`everglade-bank-lab.coax`](everglade-bank-lab.coax)). Hear and compare them at
[`/worlds/audio-lab`](https://openagents.com/worlds/audio-lab).

- Each file is trimmed, faded, normalized and encoded as 48 kHz Opus in Ogg. The loudest
  400 ms of an effect is -16 LUFS and its peak is at most -1 dBFS; the bed is -24 LUFS
  integrated ([`measure.coax`](measure.coax), BS.1770-4). The effects are mono, and the bed
  is stereo at 80 kb/s.
- The page names a file when it first plays it, and a few a frame ahead (`!fetch`), so none
  is in the Wasm. The 122 picks are 1,096,915 bytes; all 153 candidates are 1,433,518 bytes.
- They play through [`mix.coax`](mix.coax) with the levels and limits of
  [`everglade-mix.coax`](everglade-mix.coax). Add `?sfx=synth` to the page's URL to hear
  the synthesized set below instead.

### Generated: ElevenLabs Sound Effects

The `E` candidates (`e1`, `e2`, `e3`) are generated by ElevenLabs's sound-effects model
from the prompts in [`everglade-sfx.txt`](everglade-sfx.txt) (`G` lines), at prompt
influence 0.35, 0.55 and 0.75. [`everglade-sfx.manifest`](everglade-sfx.manifest) records
each one's model (`eleven_text_to_sound_v2`, or `model_elevenlabs-sound-effects-v2` through
Scenario for the first two), length, loop flag, influence and request id. The model takes
no seed, so a candidate can't be made again byte for byte; the manifest and the cached file
are the record.

Licence: generated on OpenAgents's ElevenLabs plan, whose terms allow commercial use of
the output.

Google was checked first: Vertex AI has Lyria (music) and Veo (video) but no sound-effects
model. Scenario's ElevenLabs model made the first candidates until its plan's creative units
ran out; the rest came from ElevenLabs directly. More through Scenario needs more units.

### Which won

By default a sound's picks are its best-scored candidates and those within 2 of it (scores
6 and up). Gemini 2.5 Flash on Vertex AI listens to each and scores it from 0 to 10 for its
prompt ([`everglade-sfx.scores`](everglade-sfx.scores)). The owner's picks, once made in
Audio Lab (COPY PICKS), go in `everglade-sfx.picks` and take their place.

- Spells, the bed, the raid's sounds and the rest: ElevenLabs for every first pick.
- Blows and falls: ElevenLabs first for 4 of 5; `hit-heavy` picks Kenney's heavy punch first, and
  Kenney is a variant of `hit`, `death`, `stand` and `wall-hit`.
- Footsteps: ElevenLabs first, with Kenney's steps among the variants.

### Making them again

```sh
# generate the missing candidates (the key: ELEVENLABS_API_KEY in ~/work/.secrets/elevenlabs.env)
COAX=coax/bin/coax coax/web/tool.sh aethyr/audio/sfx-tool.coax gen
# trim, normalize and encode them, and write everglade-bank.coax (DIR holds the Kenney packs unpacked)
COAX=coax/bin/coax coax/web/tool.sh aethyr/audio/sfx-tool.coax build --kenney DIR
# score the new ones on Vertex AI (the automation account's gcloud config), then build again to pick
COAX=coax/bin/coax coax/web/tool.sh aethyr/audio/sfx-tool.coax judge
```

## Everglade's synthesized effects (GitHub #12751)

Source: synthesized by Aethyr from the recipes in
[`sfx.coax`](sfx.coax) (`sfx_recipe`), with the oscillators, noise, envelopes
and filters of [`synth.coax`](synth.coax). No recording or sample library is
used. The page renders them when its audio starts, so none is downloaded.

Licence: CC0 1.0 (made by OpenAgents for this repository).

Off by default since #12832: they sound cheap, the town's bed and the footsteps too (the bed is
near-white hiss, spectral flatness 0.72 and centroid 3.9 kHz; a footstep is an 85 ms click centred
at 2.7 kHz, with none of a real step's 100 to 800 Hz body). Add `?sfx=synth` to the page's URL to hear them.

## Everglade Experimental's synthesized effects

Source: synthesized by Aethyr from the recipes in
[`experimental-sfx.coax`](experimental-sfx.coax) (`xsfx_recipe`), in the same layer format as
[`sfx.coax`](sfx.coax) and with its renderer's level. Experimental plays them for the sounds
Everglade's bank has no fitting file for; everything else it plays is the bank's picks above.
The page renders them when its audio starts (`fivelab-face.c` `coaxial_sound_bank`), so none is
downloaded.

Licence: CC0 1.0 (made by OpenAgents for this repository).

| Name | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `psychic-cast`, `psychic-hit` | Vicious Mockery: a voice-like warble, and a dissonant shudder where it lands |
| `choir` | Sacred Flame and Bless: four voices on a held "ah" |
| `rage-roar` | Rage (and a beast's roar) |
| `grunt` | Reckless Attack's grunt of effort |
| `swing-heavy` | a greataxe's or greatsword's slower, lower whoosh |
| `fizzle` | a spell that meets no one |
| `second-wind`, `surge`, `dash`, `flurry` | Second Wind and Preserve Life, Action Surge, Dash, Flurry of Blows |
| `entangle` | creaking vines |
| `imp-hurt`, `imp-death`, `gremlin-hurt`, `gremlin-death` | the monsters' own voices |
