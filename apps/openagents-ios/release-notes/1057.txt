Sign in to openagents.com with your phone: tap SIGN IN at the top right, then Scan a code and point it at the code on openagents.com/signin, and tap Approve. Your computer signs in.

On your project page, LINK A PHONE shows a code; scan it the same way and this phone works for that project. An invite link opened on the phone does the same.

The phone's key stays on this phone and comes back after a reinstall.
