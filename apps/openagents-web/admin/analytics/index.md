# Analytics

What people do on openagents.com and in the Verse playtest: sessions, the flow and where people stopped, where they came from, pages, places, devices, errors, what they said and typed, and every session's timeline.

Admins only. An admin logs in with their Nostr key: `coax/web/api/admin.sh open` in the [OpenAgents repo](https://github.com/OpenAgentsInc/openagents). Anyone else sees nothing.
