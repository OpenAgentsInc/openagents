# Chat

The OpenAgents chat, being rebuilt from nothing. For now the page is one thing: a box to type in, at the foot of the window, with Send. The box grows as you type, to eight lines, and then scrolls. Enter or Send takes what you typed and clears the box; Shift and Enter makes a new line. Nothing is sent anywhere yet, and nothing is kept.

The page before this one is at [/chat-gpu](/chat-gpu/index.md). The parts this one is built from are on [/components](/components/index.md).
