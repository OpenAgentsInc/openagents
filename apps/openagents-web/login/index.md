# Log in

Log in to openagents.com with the OpenAgents iOS app: the page shows a
one-time QR code, the app scans it and asks you to approve, and the page
logs you in with your phone's Nostr key. Nothing to type; no password.

How it works: [docs/phone-sign-in.md](https://github.com/OpenAgentsInc/openagents/blob/main/docs/phone-sign-in.md).
