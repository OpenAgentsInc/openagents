# Dead Gears

A Verse realm: hold a broken reactor against waves of mutants. Play it at [/realm/dead-gears](https://openagents.com/realm/dead-gears). This project makes it play well: aiming you control, a real character, and fights that feel good.

The tasks are on Nostr at `wss://relay.openagents.com`; this page is rewritten from them at each release.
