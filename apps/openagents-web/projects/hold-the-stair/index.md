# Hold the Stair

Tower defense with real dice: hold a wizard's stair against layered bubble-beasts that split when they pop, with a party of SRD 5e adventurers. Pitched by Marc.

Design: `docs/verse/game-ideas/hold-the-stair.md`. Play it at [/realms/hold-the-stair](https://openagents.com/realms/hold-the-stair) once the first playable lands.

The tasks are on Nostr at `wss://relay.openagents.com`; this page is rewritten from them at each release.
