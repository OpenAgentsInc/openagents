# Archery Range

You arrive on an open meadow as a Ranger with a Longbow, behind a chalk
shooting line. Five straw butts stand downrange at 10, 20, 30, 50 and 70 metres,
and two faces stand on tall poles for a shot from the air.

The bow is the same one every Everglade realm has:

- Hold the right mouse button to draw, and let go to loose. The crosshair closes
  as the draw builds and turns gold at full draw. A full draw lands where the
  crosshair points. A short draw drops short.
- The left mouse button or Escape lets the draw down. The Attack slot, or its
  number key, draws and looses at full draw.
- On a phone, hold the bow's slot to draw, drag to aim and lift to loose.

A face has ten rings. The gold in the middle scores 10 and 9, red 8 and 7,
blue 6 and 5, black 4 and 3, and white 2 and 1. An arrow on a line scores the
ring inside it. An arrow into the straw, a post or the grass is a miss.

A white horse and a red dragon wait behind the line. Walk up to one and press
**E** (or tap MOUNT) to ride, and shoot from the saddle. On the horse, W and S
go and slow, A and D turn, and Shift gallops. On the dragon, Space takes off
and climbs, Shift dives and X lands.

The tally at the top left counts your arrows, hits, accuracy and points, your
run of hits in a row, and your golds. It keeps shots on foot, from horseback
and from dragonback apart, and shows each distance you have shot from: up to
15 m, 25 m, 40 m, 60 m and beyond. The distance is from where you loosed to
the face.
