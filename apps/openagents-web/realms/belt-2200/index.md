# Belt 2200

A Verse realm at https://openagents.com/realms/belt-2200: a hard sci-fi strategy game set in the solar system in the year 2200. The Sun, Mercury to Saturn, the Moon, Ceres, Vesta and a field of Belt rocks move on their real orbits. You run the Free Belt's Hearth Station at Ceres and three ships; the Terran Compact flies its own routes, and the Mars Union mines the Belt against you.

## How to play

- Select a ship: tap or click it on the map, or its card at the bottom; on a desktop, drag a box around ships (Shift adds to the selection). On a phone, rest a finger on a ship to add it to the selection or take it out.
- Fleets: one order sends every selected ship. MINE ICE with several selected sends each on its own plan from where it is: a full hold flies home to unload, a ship already mining keeps at it, and the rest go to the ice rock each reaches soonest. Ctrl and 4 to 9 keeps the selection as a group, and the digit alone selects it again. Hold Shift as you give an order to a ship in flight and it goes there once it arrives.
- Pick where it goes: tap a world, an ice rock or the station, or press MINE ICE (the ice rock it reaches soonest) or HOME. The planner shows two transfers, GO ECONOMY and GO FAST, each with its time, delta-v and propellant; the arc is the coast between the two burns, a tick every 30 days, ending where ship and target meet on the date shown. A ship's card shows its propellant and ice in tonnes.
- A ship that reaches an ice rock mines until its hold is full, flies home, delivers its load to Hearth Station, refuels and goes back for more. A load is mostly ice, the rest metal-bearing rock. The station's refinery melts 40 t of ice a day into reaction mass (80%), water (15%) and air (5%), and ships refuel from the reaction mass. The refinery needs 30 workers; the station has 46 people, and a ship's crew is off the shifts while it is away. MASS, ICE, METAL and WORKERS at the top right are the station's.
- Orders go out from Hearth at the speed of light: a ship more than 30 light-seconds away gets its order that much later (the toast says how many minutes) and keeps to its course until then; it plans from where it is when the order arrives.
- Intercepts: when a Mars Union or Terran Compact ship's path brings it within 10,000 km of one of yours, the two are in combat range: a line joins them, the toast names them, the clock stops and the tactical view opens.
- The tactical view: the ships keep their momentum and turn at their own rate, so turning to fire costs time. Ships without a hold (KITE and the rivals) carry missiles, which burn and steer and then coast, and a rail gun that fires a slug straight out of the bow, led to where the target will be; every ship has point defense against missiles. Miners and freighters run. BREAK OFF turns ours away; 4X runs it faster. It ends when one side is out of the fight or they drift apart, and BACK TO THE MAP applies the losses: hull, crew, and a crippled ship's cargo. A crippled ship of ours is towed home; under half a hull it stays in Hearth's yard while it is mended, for metal. `?stage=battle` opens one.
- A miner leaves a rig at each rock it works, drilling 15 t of ice a day into a stock there. BUILD FREIGHTER (under the station's stores) lays down a freighter from 600 t of metal and 4 of the station's free people; it is launched at Hearth 20 days later, up to three. With three freighters built, EXPAND REFINERY takes the metal instead: each expansion refines half as much ice again a day, and each costs twice the last (500 t, then 1,000 t, 2,000 t...). A freighter can't mine: HAUL ICE sends it to the soonest rock with a rig, and it keeps hauling the stock home (2,000 t hold) with no further orders.
- The Mars Union mines from its yard on Ceres by a fixed script (no AI model): its miners fly to an ice rock, mine a hold full, leave a rig there and fly home, and its yard turns the metal into more miners, up to five. Behind you, it goes for the rocks with your rigs on them and takes the ice they have drilled (the toast says MARS UNION MINING AT the rock); well ahead, its miners rest longer at home. Its ships are red on the map.
- KITE (a warship) arriving at a rock where a Union miner works fights it there; a crippled miner limps home to Ceres and is out for two months.
- Winning: control the ice (two years running, bring home twice the Union's ice), or starve the Union's yard on Ceres (it keeps 2,000 t of ice and eats from it: cut its loads until it has gone 60 days without), or, with `?unknown=1`, hold the Unknown at Jupiter's leading Trojan point: whoever has more ships parked there holds it, and 120 days held wins it (the Union sends IRONWAKE at the start). The Union wins the mirror of the first and the last. PLAY ON closes the result and the game goes on.
- Time: PAUSE, 1, 7 or 30 days a second (Space pauses; 1, 2 and 3 pick a speed).
- The game is kept in this browser as it runs and comes back when you return. NEW (pressed twice) starts over.
- Leaving or reaching a world, a ship swings out from it, or in to it, on a hyperbola inside the world's sphere of influence before it joins its arc about the Sun.
- The map: the wheel or a pinch zooms, from the whole system down to a ship's hull; drag (or WASD and the arrows) pans; FOLLOW (or F) rides with the selected ship; with no ship selected, a tap on a world, a rock or the station flies the view to it and rides with it; SYSTEM (or C) goes back to the whole system; Escape cancels.
- Without WebGPU (a current Chrome, Edge or Safari) the page shows only its name and a link here.

## How it is made

- The orbits are the engine's: `verse/orbit.coax` (two-body Kepler motion, the planets' J2000 mean elements, spheres of influence, Lambert transfers and the rocket equation), `verse/spacecam.coax` (a floating origin in f64, so a 120 m hull and Jupiter's orbit draw with one camera) and `verse/stars.coax` (the starfield and the Sun). `docs/verse/space.md` in the OpenAgents repository lists them.
- `apps/openagents-web/belt.coax` is the game: its rules (ships, fuel, mining, the rivals' routes) and its drawing: glowing line art as on the homepage over shaded worlds (Saturn's rings, the Earth's air, Jupiter's bands) and the Belt's dust with its Kirkwood gaps. `belt-face.c` binds it for the browser and `belt.wgsl` draws it.
- The design is `docs/verse/game-ideas/belt-2200.md`. The factions, ships and places are original.
