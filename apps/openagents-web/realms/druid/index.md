# Druid

Arrive in an open meadow as a level 17 Druid. Your bar has Wild Shape and
Shapechange (Dragon). Shapechange uses your 9th-level spell slot and turns you
into an Adult Red Dragon. You keep your mind and your Hit Points, and gain the
dragon's 256 Temporary Hit Points.

As the dragon:

- Walk with W, A, S and D, or with the stick.
- Press Space or the jump button to take off. Press or hold it again to beat
  your wings and climb. Hold Shift in the air to dive. Stop beating your wings
  to glide down and land.
- Hold Fire Breath to aim and let go to breathe a 60-foot cone of fire
  (DC 21 Dexterity, 17d6 Fire, half on a success). Creatures that fail burn
  for three rounds. The breath comes back on a 5 or 6 at the start of a turn.
- Rend, Tail Sweep, Wing Buffet and Frightful Roar are your other actions.
- Return to Form ends the spell and gives you back your own shape.
