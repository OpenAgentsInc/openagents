# Everglade 5E

A Verse world at https://openagents.com/worlds/everglade-5e, not listed on the worlds page. It is Everglade, the forest city, with its spell bar on the shared SRD 5e rules engine. The city, its people, its watch, the river, the room and the way you walk are Everglade's. The bar has the same slots, pictures, keys and order. What a spell does to a creature is now the engine's: it rolls the attack against Armor Class or the saving throw, the damage dice and the conditions, and it spends a spell slot and your turn.

## Controls

They are Everglade's:

- W, A, S and D walk. Space jumps. Drag with the mouse, or hold the arrow keys, to turn the camera. The mouse wheel, or + and -, zooms it.
- Keys 1 to 9, 0, - and = use the first row of the bar. Shift, Ctrl and Alt (Option) with them use the second, third and fourth rows. You can also click or tap a slot.
- An aimed spell is picked first, then cast where you click. Esc puts it away.
- Enter opens the room's chat. M shows the map.

## How the bar plays on the SRD 5e rules

- You are a 9th-level Druid: AC 15, 57 Hit Points, spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks. One house rule gives you a 17th-level caster's spell slots (4, 3, 3, 3, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1), so every spell on the bar has a slot.
- Your turn comes every 1.5 seconds. Each turn has one action, one Bonus Action and one spell slot to spend. A spent slot comes back 9 seconds later.
- A slot that can't be used now doesn't arm or cast. When no slot of its level is left, the bar shows the seconds until one comes back. ACTION and BONUS in the panel go grey while they are spent. The number in a slot's corner is how many slots of its level you have.
- A spell resolves when Everglade's cast lands: when the cast bar ends and the meteors or the bolt fall. Its fire, smoke and lightning are Everglade's.
- The engine keeps everyone's Hit Points. Everglade's own damage roll for your spell is not used. The engine's roll lands instead, with the number over the creature's head. A guard's sword, a spear or the river hurts you through the engine, so it meets your Temporary Hit Points and your concentration.
- Everglade's people are their SRD stat blocks: Commoners, Guards, and the watch's Warrior Veterans, Mages, Knights, Priests, Archmages and Gladiators.
- The panel under your Hit Points shows your AC, save DC and attack, your turn (ACTION, BONUS, SLOT), your spell slots, your concentration and your conditions. The log under it shows each roll.
- A spell that lasts (Moonbeam, Black Tentacles, Call Lightning, Wall of Fire and others) runs again on whoever is in its area each 6-second round while you keep your concentration. Putting it away ends your concentration.

## With others

The room is this world's own. Each player's Hit Points, conditions and casts go to the others in the room on its chat, in lines the chat panel doesn't show, about every 2.5 seconds. They show over each player's head. Healing Word and Mass Cure Wounds can choose a hurt friend.

## How it is made

- `aethyr/rules/srd/` is the engine. `docs/dnd/README.md` is its specification. `aethyr/rules/srd/more.coax` holds the SRD records Everglade's bar needs, and `aethyr/rules/srd/house.coax` its four house rules and how Everglade's numbers became them.
- `apps/openagents-web/fivebar.coax` is the hotbar layer shared with 5E MMO: each slot of Everglade's bar as an entry, whether it can be used now, and its use.
- `apps/openagents-web/everglade5e-rules.coax` runs the engine for this world. It is its own C unit.
- `apps/openagents-web/everglade5e.coax` is the page: Everglade's page (`everglade.coax`) as it is, with the layer between it and the rules, and the 5E HUD.
- `apps/openagents-web/everglade5e-face.c` binds them for the browser. It is Everglade's face, with the rules beside it.
