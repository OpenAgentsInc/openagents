# Everglade Experimental

Explore the Character Create glade with Everglade's character controller.

The REST controls below the class and level selector offer Short Rest, Sleep,
Watch, Hit Die and End Rest. Stop moving and settle on the ground before
starting. Watch wakes you; Sleep makes you Unconscious and unable to move or
act. Damage, Initiative and leveled spells can interrupt Long Rest.

A Short Rest takes at least an hour. When it finishes, choose Hit Die to spend
one die at a time for healing. A Long Rest takes at least eight hours, including
six hours of sleep. Time continues in the world, so nearby monsters can interrupt
your rest. Ending rest does not remove the 16-hour wait after a completed Long Rest.
