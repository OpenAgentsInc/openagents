# Everglade II

Walk out of Character Create's glade along one winding path through dense woods. The path ends in a clearing.

- W, A, S and D walk. Shift walks instead of running. Space jumps.
- Drag to look around. Scroll to zoom. The arrow keys turn the camera.
- On a touch screen, the left thumb walks and the right thumb looks.
- You walk the character you saved in Character Create. With none saved, you walk the studio's default.
- Up to 16 players share a room. When a room is full, you join another and the page says which.
