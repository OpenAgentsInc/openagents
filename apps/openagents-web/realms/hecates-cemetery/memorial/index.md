# A memorial in Hecate's Cemetery

Each pet placed in Hecate's Cemetery has a memorial page of its own at https://openagents.com/memorial/ and its eight-letter name, the link its owner shares: its name, dates and line on a tombstone, the photos and videos its owner shows, its story, what made it itself, what it did, and the notes from those who knew it, with the way into the garden to visit its tombstone and leave a note. This address is the page's shell; a memorial's own address fills it.
