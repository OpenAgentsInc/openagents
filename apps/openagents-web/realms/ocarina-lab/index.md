# Ocarina Lab

Explore a winding forest path with Link, then inspect his recovered materials and original animation clips.

**WASD** moves, **Shift** walks, **Space** jumps, **B** plays the backflip, and dragging or the arrow keys turns the camera. Touch uses the shared left movement and right look controls; tap **JUMP** to jump.

**C** inspects the character, **H** frames his face, and **V** switches material views. In inspection, drag to orbit, use **[ ]** to select a clip, **P** to pause, and **, .** to step frames. **R** returns to the forest. **1** opens [Animation Lab](/worlds/animation-lab); [Valley Lab](/worlds/valley-lab) is the source inspection reference.

Link's mesh, rig, skin weights, textures and clips come from the local source pack. The forest and its collision fixtures are original Metaverse Engine geometry. Source assets remain owner-local and are absent from the repository and public builds. A source pack must be available locally for this realm to load.

Materials use the original texture aliases, UV sets, normal channels, opacity maps and material parameters. Lighting and special eye, scattering and hair responses are Metaverse Engine translations; original game rendering, cloth and hair simulation are not claimed. The lab controller uses shared Metaverse Engine walking and collision, rather than the original game code.
