# Splice Bots — Unreal jam build

Splice Bots, played in the jam build's levels: the solar farm, the plain, the
garage ring and the lab, brought over from Unreal by the Metaverse Engine's
level importer (`metaverse-engine/tools/unreal-level-import.coax`, GitHub
#13648). It is the same game as `/worlds/splice-bots`
(`metaverse/splice-bots/splice-bots.coax`), put in the jam world
(`metaverse/splice-bots/jam/jam-world.coax`) and drawn in its cel and ink pass
(`metaverse/splice-bots/splice-jam.coax`, GitHub #13653).

The controls are the game's: **WASD** or the arrows to move, **E** to use,
**F** to drill, and the mouse to look.

The level pack is private and served locally only (`?pack=splice-bots-jam`);
the game's files are not in the repository. Without the pack the game plays in
its own Mars world.

To walk the jam's levels on their own, with no game, open the importer's walker
(`apps/openagents-web/importworld.coax`) on the same pack.
