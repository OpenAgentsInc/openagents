# Valley Lab: Ancient World

An unlisted, local source-world slice inside Valley Lab. The licensed source pack
stays in the shared private Aethyrforge cache. The roughly 250 metre basin holds
27,501 original placements of 130 mesh types, with 240 texture layers including Echo. Open with `?pack=valley-world`.

WASD moves Echo, Shift walks, Space jumps, dragging or the arrows looks, and R
resets. Keys 2–5 visit four parts of the basin. C inspects Echo, H inspects her
face, V changes material diagnostics, and 1 returns to the lab stations.

Original scenery placements and texture maps are imported through Unreal's SDK.
The renderer uses Aethyr's lighting; source Nanite fallback meshes and sampled
ground collision have separate fidelity limits recorded in the verification log.
