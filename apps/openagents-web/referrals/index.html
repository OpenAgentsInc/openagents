Referral & Promo Credit Program

OpenAgents promotional credit lets developers try hosted OpenAgents inference and reward friends who join. Promo credit is strictly a non-withdrawable, non-transferable service discount — paid Bitcoin/Lightning credit in satoshis remains separate and is the only money on OpenAgents.

2. GitHub Eligibility

  • Your GitHub account must be at least 90 days old.
  • It must show public developer activity: at least 3 public non-fork repositories, or 5 followers, or membership in at least 1 public organization.
  • Each numeric GitHub account ID can claim promotional credit once across OpenAgents. Unlinking or deleting an OpenAgents account does not reset GitHub eligibility.
  • OpenAgents requests a read-only GitHub check and never stores your GitHub OAuth token in our database.

3. How Promo Credit Spends & Expires

  • Promo-eligible models only: Promo credit applies automatically to designated OpenAgents models (currently google/gemini-4-neon and openagents/router-decision). Other models debit your paid Bitcoin credit.
  • Spend order: On eligible models, promo credit spends automatically before paid Bitcoin credit, consuming the lot with the earliest expiry date first.
  • Daily cap: At most $5.00 of promo credit may be spent per account per UTC day. Additional usage on the same day falls through seamlessly to paid Bitcoin credit.
  • Expiry: Promotional bonus grants expire 90 days after grant. Lifetime kickback promo credit stays active as long as your account has activity at least once every 365 days.
  • Not money: Promo credit has no cash value, cannot be withdrawn to Bitcoin or fiat, cannot be transferred between accounts, and cannot be used for marketplace author payouts or third-party disbursements.

4. Fair Use, Caps & Clawbacks

  • Self-referrals, reciprocal referrals between two accounts, shared GitHub accounts, and automated farming are prohibited.
  • Inviter bonuses are capped at 20 activated invitees per rolling 30-day window by default (higher limits may be granted to verified community ambassadors).
  • Suspicious bursts may be held for review. If fraud or abuse is confirmed, OpenAgents may void pending rewards and claw back unspent promo credit only. Paid Bitcoin credit in your balance is never clawed back for referral enforcement.
  • OpenAgents may pause, adjust, or end promotional offers or daily/total program budgets at any time. Rewards already granted remain governed by the terms version recorded on the grant.
