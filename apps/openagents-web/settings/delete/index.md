# Delete your account

[openagents.com/settings/delete](https://openagents.com/settings/delete) deletes your account.
Log in first. Deleting needs your key, passkey or recovery code from the last 5 minutes; on an
account with only email, a new email code from the last 5 minutes.

## What happens

1. Your account is frozen at once: every login ends, your agents pause and nothing can be spent.
2. 30 days later, we delete your emails, keys, agents, realms, saves, chats and files. The page
   shows the date and time.
3. Until then, log in any way and choose CANCEL DELETION.

Before you delete:

- **Service credit.** The page shows any credit left. It can't be paid out: spend it first, or
  it goes with the account.
- **Your wallet.** Your Bitcoin wallet lives on your devices, not with us. Write down its
  recovery words first: we can't restore it.
- **Your data.** Download it first at [/settings/data](/settings/data/index.md).

## What we keep

- Payments made to us: amounts and dates, no longer linked to you, as the law requires.
- Your security log's entries, without your account id.
- The account id, so it is never given to anyone else.
- Any Lightning Address name that was ever paid. We hold it so that payments meant for you never
  reach someone else.

## Backups

Our nightly backups are locked and can't be deleted for 35 days. Your data leaves the last of
them by day 40 after it is deleted.
