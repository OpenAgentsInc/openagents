# Settings

Your settings, for someone logged in. The page sits beside the side, with its sections down its left. Each section has its own address:

- [Account](/settings/account): who is logged in (your emails and keys), Log out, and the pages that hold the rest of your account: [security](/settings/security/index.md), [devices](/settings/devices/index.md), [agents](/settings/agents/index.md), [wallet](/settings/wallet/index.md), [Lightning Address](/settings/lightning/index.md), [your data](/settings/data/index.md) and [deleting your account](/settings/delete/index.md).
- [Agents and models](/settings/models): your Claude account (an Anthropic API key or a Claude subscription token, checked, sealed and never shown again), whether the agents on this computer are on in this browser and which are ready, and the agent and model a new chat starts with.
- [Appearance](/settings/appearance): whether the side is shown. Motion follows your system's setting.
- [Limits](/settings/limits): the chat's limits and every limit we enforce on logging in. All of them are on [/docs/limits](/docs/limits/index.md).
- [About](/settings/about): the release this page is from (its commit and the day it was built), the source code, and the documentation.

What a setting keeps is kept in this browser, except your Claude account, which is kept on your account.
