# Enter the Verse

The way into the Verse playtest at https://openagents.com/verse, as playtest.openagents.com drew it: CREATE YOUR WORLD, ENTER THE VERSE, one line, "Edit our haunted house. Can you make it scarier?", and **PLAYTEST NOW**, which opens the Haunted House directly, [/realm/haunted-house](https://openagents.com/realm/haunted-house). The galaxy of worlds is still at [/verse/galaxy](https://openagents.com/verse/galaxy). Nothing is asked.

Behind it, faint grey worlds pull on each other. Press and hold in the stars to make one: it grows while held, joins the rest when let go, bounces once off another and then merges with it in a burst of sparks.

What you do here is recorded as the playtest recorded it, with an anonymous id: the visit, the page's load time, what you press, and which way you went in (`landing`, `load-landing`, `click`, `start`). Someone who gave the old playtest an email in this browser is joined again under it (`join`).

Drawn by Coax with WebGPU: `apps/openagents-web/verse.coax` (the page, its orbs and what a press does), `verse-face.c` (its binding for the browser) and `verse.wgsl` (the playtest's field of stars, the PLAYTEST NOW glass and the plates). Without WebGPU (a current Chrome, Edge or Safari) the page shows only its name and a link here.
