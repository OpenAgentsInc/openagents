Aethyr Grid port

OpenAgents Grid art, robot animation samples, layout, NIP-MV presence and
NIP-DEPLOY behavior are adapted from OpenAgentsInc/openagents. The pinned
source revision and scene hash are in assets/grid-source.json. The Grid's
source runtime was Rust/wgpu; this port uses Aethyr's C++/WebGL2 renderer,
retains Aethyr's controller, omits zone portals, and implements service
panels in JavaScript with a read-only Python status bridge.

OpenAgents source is distributed under Apache-2.0; see
openagents-apache-2.0.txt. The original Grid robot's admitted sources are
CC0-1.0; see grid-robot-cc0.txt. Its sampled Universal Animation Library
animations are Quaternius CC0 assets, as are Aethyr's existing characters.

web/grid-nostr.js is the unmodified browser bundle from nostr-tools 2.25.2
(npm package nostr-tools@2.25.2, package/lib/nostr.bundle.js). Public domain
under the Unlicense: nostr-tools.txt. Its cryptographic dependencies
include Paul Miller's @noble libraries and @scure/base, MIT licensed; their
notices are provided in noble-mit.txt, noble-hashes-mit.txt and
scure-base-mit.txt.

The scene contract decoder includes Nanopb; asset verification includes PicoSHA2.
See engine-proto.txt for their licenses and notices.
