# OpenAgents Coder for iPhone privacy

Updated October 16, 2026.

This notice describes OpenAgents Coder for iPhone and its paired Coder Desktop
app. It supplements the [OpenAgents Privacy Policy](https://openagents.com/privacy).
The optional saved-chat feature in older Coder apps is separate from this
phone app's sync service.

When you sign in and use sync, OpenAgents stores your account and device
identifiers, device names, session titles, messages, attachments, and session
status on its servers so your devices can share them. Your account controls
access to these sessions. Network connections use encryption. This service
does not provide end-to-end encrypted chat storage.

Your account service processes your sign-in name and email. If you choose to
use voice, audio reaches the model provider through your connected computer.
Your computer may also send session content to the coding or model provider
you choose. Those providers' privacy terms apply to their processing.

The app uses local storage for your login, cached sessions, and preferences.
Signing out removes the phone's saved login and local chat cache. It does
not delete synced content from your account. Deleting a session removes it
from session lists; stored history and backups follow the retention terms
in the OpenAgents Privacy Policy.

The phone app does not use advertising identifiers, advertising SDKs, or
cross-app tracking. Push notifications are unavailable in the first release.

For access or deletion requests, contact **chris@openagents.com**. The general
policy explains retention, service providers, account choices, and contact
information.
