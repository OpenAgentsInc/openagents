# The Grid robot: provenance

The Grid robot is an original character for Verse, a black-and-white robot
that patrols the Grid's plaza as an NPC (`docs/verse/grid-robot.md`). Every
file here is generated by script.

| File | Made by |
| --- | --- |
| `build/grid-robot.<variant>.glb` (not committed) | `scripts/blender/grid_robot.py` in Blender 5.2.2 LTS, headless: `Blender -b --factory-startup --python scripts/blender/grid_robot.py -- <variant>` |
| `grid-robot.<variant>.gltf`, `.bin`, `manifest.json`, `license.txt` | `python3 scripts/blender/grid_robot_admit.py`, from the build glb files |

Modes, per the asset runbook:

- **Reference** for every part: the helmet, visor, crest, ear pods, chest,
  emblem, abdomen, belt, pauldrons, limbs, joints, hands, and boots are
  lofted from primitives by the script, with four flat materials and no
  image.
- **Skeleton:** the Universal rig of `Superhero_Male_FullBody.gltf` from
  Quaternius's Universal Base Characters (CC0 1.0, retained under
  `assets/verse/characters/quaternius/`, its license pinned in that folder's
  manifest), whose 65 joints' names, parents, and rest transforms the
  admission copies verbatim. Each part is bound rigidly to one joint.

Reference studied: "Low Poly Sci-fi Robot" by GarderX (Fab, standard
license, which forbids redistribution). Inspired by, nothing copied: it was
opened in Blender only to render study views and measure part sizes, for
proportions, its breakdown into rigid parts, and its design language. None
of its geometry, UVs, or materials were copied, edited, or exported, and the
robot's visor, chest emblem, and panel layout are its own.
