# Game-icons.net action icons

These icons come from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net), as white SVGs
on a transparent background, the same files the previous engine's hotbars
drew (`git show 0b401561a0^:assets/verse/icons/game-icons/CREDITS.md`). They
are licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/);
`license.txt` is the archive's license file. The files are unmodified.
`verse/tools/everglade_hotbar.py` rasterizes and tints them for Everglade's
spell bar, as the old engine did (`crates/verse-content/src/compiler/icons.rs`).

Icons made by Lorc (<http://lorcblog.blogspot.com>):

- `lorc/meteor-impact.svg` (Meteor Swarm)
- `lorc/lightning-frequency.svg` (Thunderbolt, in white-gold, and Mega
  Thunderbolt, in electric violet)
- `lorc/frozen-orb.svg` (Water Orb)
- `lorc/whirlwind.svg` (Wind Wall)
- `lorc/feather.svg` (Feather Fall)
- `lorc/wind-slap.svg` (Gust of Wind)
- `lorc/sonic-boom.svg` (Thunderwave)

Icons made by Delapouite (<https://delapouite.com>):

- `delapouite/house.svg` (Rebuild)
- `delapouite/gravitation.svg` (Reverse Gravity, drawn upside down)
- `delapouite/body-height.svg` (Levitate)
