# Deferred browser water timers

All eight dry and wet Water Lab and Everglade cases pass on WebGPU and
WebGL2. Water Lab WebGPU retains 728 valid samples (2.104 ms mean);
Everglade retains 613 (0.339 ms). WebGL2 has no GPU timestamps.
The four wet captures show water and are inspected; images remain private.
The frozen test-derived module verifies the timer change before calibration
and rebase. It is not a production module or phone qualification.
