You're stuck in the Ice Cream Man's house, and he's coming.

Find the three keys, press the sprinkle buttons in order, and get out before he catches you. Jump over his sprinkles, melt his minions, and fight back with Hot Fudge, the Fart Launcher, a Candy Cane Sword, a Waffle Cone Spear and more. Freeze him, or melt him into a puddle of vanilla, if you can.

- A bright, silly 3D chase you can finish in a few minutes, and want to beat again
- Touch controls built for iPhone and iPad: a stick to walk, drag to look, big buttons to jump, sprint and fire
- Game controllers and keyboards work too
- Third person or first person, with a shoulder camera for aiming
- A short tutorial, and your best time saved on your device
- Plays fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, no tracking

The Ice Cream Man is a cartoon chase. If he catches you, he lunges at the screen with a loud sting and a splat of strawberry syrup. Spooky, never gory.
