# The Ice Cream Man: App Store answers

Publisher: OpenAgents, Inc. Bundle ID: com.openagents.icecreamman.
SKU: icecreamman-ios. Primary language: English (U.S.).

## Category

Primary: Games, subcategory Action (second subcategory: Adventure).

## App Privacy

Data Not Collected. The app makes no network requests: the game runs from
files inside the app, and the only things it stores are on the device, in
the web view's local storage (settings, best time, whether the tutorial was
seen). There are no analytics, ads, accounts, or third-party SDKs. Links in
the game ("Read the full guide", OpenAgents home) open Safari; they are not
loaded in the app.

Privacy policy URL: https://openagents.com/privacy

## Age rating questionnaire

- Cartoon or Fantasy Violence: Infrequent/Mild (you throw hot fudge and
  candy weapons at a cartoon ice cream man and his minions; they melt or
  freeze, no blood).
- Realistic Violence: None.
- Prolonged Graphic or Sadistic Realistic Violence: None.
- Profanity or Crude Humor: Infrequent/Mild (a "Fart Launcher" weapon and
  fart sound jokes).
- Mature/Suggestive Themes: None.
- Horror/Fear Themes: Infrequent/Mild. When he catches you, the death
  screen is a cartoon jumpscare: the Ice Cream Man lunges at the camera with
  a loud sting and a red strawberry-syrup splatter. No gore.
- Medical/Treatment Information, Alcohol/Tobacco/Drugs, Simulated Gambling,
  Sexual Content or Nudity, Graphic Sexual Content: None.
- Contests: No. Unrestricted Web Access: No (links open in Safari).
  User-generated content / messaging: No. Gambling: No. Loot boxes: No.
  In-app purchases: No. Advertising: No. Parental controls: No. Age
  assurance: No.

Expected result: 9+ (mild horror/fear and mild cartoon violence).

## Kids Category: not chosen

The Kids Category would require no links out of the app without a parental
gate and no external links in the main UI; the game's cards link to the web
guide and to openagents.com, and the jumpscare makes it a poor fit for
under-9s anyway. Listing it as an ordinary 9+ game keeps the links and the
jumpscare honest. To move into Kids later, the links would need a parental
gate and the jumpscare a softer variant.

## Export compliance

ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is false in Info.plist: the app uses no
encryption beyond what iOS itself provides.

## Review notes (paste into App Review Information > Notes)

No sign-in or account. The game opens on a four-card tutorial (Skip or
Next); then walk with the stick on the left, drag on the right to look, and
use the Jump, Sprint, Fire and Use buttons. Find three keys to escape. A
game controller also works (A jump, right trigger fire, menu button for the
pause menu). Everything runs offline from the app bundle.
