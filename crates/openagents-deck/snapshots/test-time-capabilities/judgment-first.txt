  Cheap judgment before expensive thinking
body at 40,176 720x170
  - Decide first whether the turn needs a large model at all. Offer a ladder at rising cost: a prepared answer, one a small model finishes, a grounded one, the full model, an agent with a computer.
  - Never wrong fast. A prepared answer is served only when its readings clear thresholds tuned for precision. A fast answer to the wrong question is worse than a slow right one.
  - Capability can substitute for compute. A tool that answers directly can beat a model that has to search for the answer, on time and on correctness.
