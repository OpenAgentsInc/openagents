                Term               What it names
    ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    Admit       Admission          A locked component let into one run

    Measure     Delta              With minus without, same tests, repeated

    Use         Reach, restraint   Used where it helps, withheld where not

    Allocate    Judgment budget    Deciding costs far less than answering

    Reproduce   Reproduced         Someone else reran it: compatible result

    Validate    Validated          Helps on tests its author didn't write

    Adopt       Adoption           Joins the defaults, measured against them

    Credit      Credit             For reruns either way, and for adoption
