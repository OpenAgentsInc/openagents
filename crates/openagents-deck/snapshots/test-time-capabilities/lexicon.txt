    Admit      Admission          A locked version of a component allowed into one run

    Measure    Delta              With arm minus without arm, same tests, repeated

    Use        Reach, restraint   Uses it when a test says to, leaves it alone otherwise

    Allocate   Judgment budget    Deciding how to answer costs far less than answering

    Verify     Verified           Someone else reran it and got the same verdict

    Share      Adoption, credit   It joins everyone's defaults; its authors get credit
