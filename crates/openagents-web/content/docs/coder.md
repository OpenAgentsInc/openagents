# Coder

Coder is our coding agent. It runs on your own computer, in your own
projects, and uses that computer's own git and GitHub sign-in.

## Before Coder can run

On your Mac:

1. [Connect the Mac](/docs/connect-a-computer) to your phone.
2. Sign in to **Codex** or **Claude Code** in the Mac's terminal. Coder
   uses the one you're signed in to. OpenAgents for Mac shows whether each
   is signed in.
3. In OpenAgents for Mac, click **Choose folder…** to pick a project, and
   turn on **Let my phone start Coder here**.

## Running it

From a chat on your phone, send the work to your computer. Coder takes the
conversation as its task, works in the project, and shows each step in the
chat. You can follow along, and the work keeps going if you close the app.

If the computer is asleep or offline, the message waits on your phone and
goes out as soon as the computer is back. It is never sent twice.
