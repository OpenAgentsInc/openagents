# Connect a computer

Connect your Mac once, and your phone can reach it from anywhere.

1. Open **OpenAgents** on your Mac. It shows a QR code.
2. On your iPhone, point the **Camera** at the code and tap the
   OpenAgents banner. Or, in the app, open **Account**, then
   **Computers**, then **Connect a computer**, and scan the code.
3. Both screens say the computer is connected.

That's it. There is no terminal step, no account to sign in to, and no
key to copy. You don't need Tailscale.

## Can't scan?

Click **Can't scan? Copy a code instead** on the Mac and paste the code
into the app. A code works once, for one phone, for a short time.

## Near each other

When your phone and Mac are on the same network, the app can also find
the Mac nearby. Both screens show the same six-digit number; click
**Connect** on the Mac if they match.

## What a connected phone can do

A connected phone can do anything you can do on that computer: chat with
it, run Coder in your projects, open a terminal, and see and manage the
phones connected to it.

To take a phone's access away, click **Remove** next to it in OpenAgents
for Mac.

## Anywhere

Your phone connects straight to the Mac when it can, and through the
OpenAgents relay when it can't, so it works on the same Wi-Fi and on
mobile data.
