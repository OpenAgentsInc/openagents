# Start on the web

OpenAgents runs in your browser. Start with [the homepage](/new-lander),
[the Verse](/new-lander/verse), or [a new chat](/chat).

## Explore and create

1. Choose **Enter the Verse** on the homepage.
2. Continue as a guest to explore the planets and enter the Haunted House.
3. Choose **Edit** to open the tools and try a change.
4. Sign in when prompted to save, publish, and share your work.

## Chat

Open [chat](/chat), type a question or an idea, and send it.
See [Chat on openagents.com](/docs/website) for the chat controls.

Older app and installer links now lead here. Use the web experience on
both your phone and computer.
