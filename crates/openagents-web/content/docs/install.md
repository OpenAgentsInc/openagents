# Install

You need two things: the app on your Mac and the app on your iPhone. The
[install page](/install) has the download links.

1. **Mac.** Download OpenAgents for Mac, open the `.dmg`, and drag
   **OpenAgents** onto **Applications**. It needs macOS 13 or later and
   runs on Apple silicon and Intel.
2. **iPhone.** The app is in testing. Join it on TestFlight from the
   [install page](/install).
3. **Connect them.** Open OpenAgents on the Mac and scan its QR code with
   your phone. See [Connect a computer](/docs/connect-a-computer).

OpenAgents for Mac updates itself. The iPhone app updates through
TestFlight.

Android is in testing and not public yet. OpenAgents for Linux and Windows
is not published yet.
