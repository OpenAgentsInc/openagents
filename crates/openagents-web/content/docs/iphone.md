# OpenAgents for iPhone

OpenAgents for iPhone is the OpenAgents app on your phone. Ask anything,
start coding work that runs in our cloud, and connect your own computer
when you want to. You don't need a computer or an account to start.

The app is in beta on TestFlight, Apple's app for beta apps. Get
[TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) from the
App Store first. The public beta link goes on the
[download page](/download) once Apple approves the current build for
outside testers. It isn't in the App Store yet.

## No sign-in

The first time the app opens, it makes your account on the phone: an ID
made from 12 words that the app keeps on this phone. There's nothing to
sign up for. **Settings** shows your ID under **Your ID**.

The same 12 words also open your [wallet](/docs/wallet), so one backup
covers both. Write the words down when the wallet asks you to, and keep
them offline. Anyone with them has your account and your bitcoin.

An app that was already signed in keeps its account and chats.

## Chats

The app opens on a new chat. Send a question, and the answer streams in.

- **The sessions list** holds your chats: chats in our cloud, and the
  sessions on each computer you connected. Pin, copy the transcript
  of, or archive one from its **…** menu.
- **Where it runs:** **Run on** picks where coding work runs: **OpenAgents Cloud** or one of
  your computers.
- **Agent** picks who answers: OpenAgents, or one of your
  [custom agents](/docs/custom-agents).

## Coding work in our cloud

Ask for a coding task with a project picked, and it runs on a cloud
computer we start for you, with OpenAgents models, so you need no other
key. New accounts get free cloud time to try it. **Settings → Start
OpenAgents Cloud** starts it ahead of time, and **Stop OpenAgents Cloud**
stops it.

When the free time is used up, the app says so. To keep going, top up on
[openagents.com](https://openagents.com/settings) or get Pro; see [Pricing](/docs/pricing).

To run Claude Code in the cloud, add your own Claude key in
[Settings](https://openagents.com/settings) on openagents.com.

## Your computers

**Settings → Connect a computer** opens the scanner. Scan the code your
computer shows, and the computer joins your account and signs in as you.
Its sessions then show in the sessions list. See
[Connect a computer](/docs/connect-a-computer).

## Voice

With a connected computer, **Settings → Voice** picks the voice agent and
its voice. A call can send a request to any saved agent and chat you
choose. Each computer's voice conversation shows in your sessions as
**Voice transcript**.

## Settings

- **Your ID**, **Project sharing**, and **Wallet**.
- **Devices:** **Connect a computer**, your computers with **Online** or
  **Offline**, and **OpenAgents Cloud**.
- **Voice**, **Appearance** (System, Light, or Dark), and **Wallpaper**.
- **Sessions:** **Group by Project** and **Archived Sessions**.
- **About** shows the version and build.
- **Sign Out** and **Delete Account**.
