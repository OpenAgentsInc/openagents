# Pricing

**Free** is OpenAgents on your own computer: Coder in front of the agents
you already use (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Grok Build, Devin CLI,
Cursor), running on your own models, keys and subscriptions. We never
charge for those; you pay their providers as you do today. Chatting with
OpenAgents on the web and your phone is free too, and Coder's `auto` model
comes with a free daily allowance (below).

**Pro** ($20 a month) is for work that runs on our machines instead of
yours: cloud environments and cloud agents that keep working with your
laptop closed, model usage included each month, uploads, advanced
projects, and CAD Lab.

**Enterprise** is Pro for a company, deployed where you need it
([below](#enterprise)).

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runs on | Your computer | Your computer and our cloud |
| Agents and models | Your own, at no charge from us | Your own, plus model usage included |
| Coder `auto` | Free daily allowance | Free daily allowance, plus usage included |
| Cloud environments and agents | No | Yes |
| CAD Lab | No | Yes |
| Price | $0 | $20 a month |

## Free daily allowance

When you're logged in, Coder's `auto` model is free up to about $1 of model
use a day. When today's allowance is used up, Coder tells you and keeps
going on your own keys or subscriptions, or on credit you add. The
allowance starts again each day at midnight UTC.

## Pro

Pro is for cloud environments: a repository set up, built, checked, and
saved on our machines so your tasks can start from it. It isn't on sale
yet. When it is, **Settings → Plan** shows how to subscribe.

| | Pro |
| --- | --- |
| Price | $20 a month |
| Hours | 100 machine-hours a month |
| Machine | 2 vCPU, 8 GB |
| At once | 2 machines |
| CAD Lab | Design parts by talking, measure them, and compare versions |
| Saved environments | 20 GB, up to 10 saved versions |
| Run length | No limit from us; set your own if you want one |
| Models | Your own Claude or Codex key or subscription |

Unused hours don't roll over to the next month.

Without Pro, designs you already made in CAD Lab stay yours to open and
download; making or changing a part needs Pro.

## Extra hours

When the month's 100 hours run out, setups stop until the next month
starts. To keep going, turn on **Extra hours** in **Settings → Plan** and
set a monthly limit. Extra hours are $0.18 a machine-hour, paid from your
credits, and never more than the limit you set. They're off until you turn
them on.

**Settings → Plan** shows the hours you've used this month, your saved
storage, and your extra-hours limit.
