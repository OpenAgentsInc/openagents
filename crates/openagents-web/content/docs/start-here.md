# Start here

Start with [OpenAgents in your browser](/new-lander).

1. Choose **Enter the Verse** to explore connected worlds.
2. Continue as a guest and visit the Haunted House.
3. Choose **Edit** to try a change. Sign in when prompted to keep your work.
4. Save, publish, and share your world. Return to the planets to create your own.

For a conversation with an agent, open [chat](/chat).
