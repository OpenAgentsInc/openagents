# The Verse

The Verse is a shared world inside the app. Its Verse tab (the globe)
puts you in the Grid: a floor of white lines with other players walking
around live, a ball, blocks, and the Gym.

## Moving around

- **Walk:** push the faint stick at the bottom left.
- **Look:** push the stick at the bottom right, or drag anywhere.
- **Jump:** double-tap.
- **Zoom:** pinch.

Walk into the ball to push it. Everything in the Grid is shared: other
players see it move, and it stays where it was left.

OpenAgents for Mac shows the Grid from above behind its screens.
