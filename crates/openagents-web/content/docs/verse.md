# The Verse

The Verse is a shared world you open in your browser, with nothing to
install. Its places run on this site:

- **[The Grid](/grid):** a floor of white lines where other players walk
  around live, with a ball, a stack of blocks, dominoes, and the Gym. See
  [The Grid](/docs/the-grid) for a picture.
- **[Everglade](/everglade):** a forest glade with a small workshop.
- **[The Grove](/druid):** a druid training field.

The first download is large; your browser keeps it after that. The Verse
isn't in the phone app.

## Move around

- **W** and **S** move forward and back; **A** and **D** (or **Q** and
  **E**) step sideways. **Space** jumps; **Shift** sprints.
- Hold the right mouse button to look and turn; hold the left to orbit.
  The wheel zooms, into first person and back.

## Shared things

In the Grid, walk into the ball to push it; it rolls with real physics.
Everyone shares the ball, the blocks, and the dominoes, and finds them
where they were left. What other players see can lag a little.

## Who sees you

While you're in the Grid, your browser shares only your avatar's
position and the shared things. Other players see a name tag of your
key's first eight characters. Leaving the page closes the connection.
