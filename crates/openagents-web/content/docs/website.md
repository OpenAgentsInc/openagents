# Chat on openagents.com

The [new chat page](/chat) has a message box. Type what you want to ask and send it.
The site opens a new chat at an address under `/chat/`.

`Enter` sends the message. `Ctrl+Enter` (`⌘+Enter` on a Mac) queues it for
later, and `Shift+Enter` inserts a line. The hint under the message box says
so: "Enter to send, Ctrl+Enter to queue for later".

Queued messages show above the [message box](/chat). Click one to change its words,
or press **Remove** to drop it. They send in order, one after each answer.

`Enter` (or **Send**) while a reply is being written stops that reply and
sends your message now. What the reply wrote so far stays in the chat, with
"You stopped this answer." under it. Use `Ctrl+Enter` instead to let the
reply finish and send your message after it.

Without scripts, use **Send**. The [download](/download) link still works
either way.

## Log in

You can chat without logging in. To keep your chats in your account, go
to [Log in](https://openagents.com/login). There are three ways:

- **Scan with your phone.** The page shows a code; scan it with
  [OpenAgents for iPhone](/docs/iphone) and approve.
- **A browser extension** that holds your Nostr key.
- **GitHub**, for accounts that signed up with GitHub before. New
  accounts don't use GitHub to log in; you can still connect it for your
  repositories.

Chats you started before logging in move to your account.

## What it doesn't do

The website chat does not start Coder or open a screen. It does show
Coder's chats from a computer you
[connected to your account](/docs/connect-a-computer), and a reply there
reaches Coder while it's open on that computer. For more, use
[OpenAgents Terminal](/docs/terminal) or
[OpenAgents for iPhone](/docs/iphone).
