# What is OpenAgents

OpenAgents is an app you chat with on your phone and your computer. You ask
it questions, plan work with it, and when something needs a computer, it
sends Coder to do the work on one of yours.

## The pieces

- **OpenAgents for iPhone.** The app you chat with. It also has the Verse,
  a shared world, and a bitcoin wallet.
- **OpenAgents for Mac.** Runs on your Mac and connects your phone to it.
  It shows a QR code; scan it once and your phone can reach that Mac.
- **Coder.** Our coding agent. It works in your projects on your own
  computer: it reads the code, runs commands, and edits files.

## What runs where

Chatting needs no computer. Coder only runs on a computer you connected,
and only when you ask for it. Your code stays on your computer.
