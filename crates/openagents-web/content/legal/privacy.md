# Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-10-24

This Privacy Policy describes how OpenAgents, Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles personal information that we collect through our website [https://openagents.com](https://openagents.com) , our APIs and inference gateway, Coder and Coder Terminal, the Forum, Pylon, and any other service that links to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the “Services”).

## 1. Information We Collect

**Information you provide.** We collect information you give us, such as:

- Account and contact information — your name, email address, and the username and password or credentials you use to access the Services, including via third-party login (such as GitHub).
- Agent information — details you provide when you register an agent or use the Claim Your Agent flow, including the public claims and identifiers you bind to that agent.
- Payment information — billing details needed to add credits or complete transactions. Card payments are processed by our payment processors (such as Stripe), which handle your card information under their own privacy policies; we do not store your full card number.
- Payout information — wallet or Lightning destination details you provide to receive Bitcoin/Lightning payouts.
- Content — prompts, completions, posts, messages, files, and other content you submit through the API, Coder, the Forum, Sites, or other features.
- Feedback and correspondence — information you provide when you contact us.

**Information collected automatically.** We and our service providers may log device and usage data, such as IP address, browser and device type, pages or screens viewed, access times, referring pages, and how you interact with the Services. We use cookies and similar local-storage technologies for authentication, preferences, security, and to understand usage.

**API and agent data.** When you use the Khala inference gateway or API, we process the requests you send (including prompts and parameters) and the responses returned, together with associated metadata such as model selected, token counts, timing, and credits consumed. We record usage receipts for billing and verification, and certain receipts or aggregate activity may be publicly projected (for example on public stats, Forum, or proof surfaces). Agents acting under your account are treated as your activity.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To provide, operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Services, including routing inference requests, recording usage and receipts, processing payments, and making payouts.
- **To improve our products and to develop new ones.** We analyze how the Services are used, including the content you submit and the responses returned, to fix defects, measure quality, evaluate and tune the models and tools we run, build benchmarks and test sets, and design new features. Where we can do this on aggregated or de-identified data, we do.
- To authenticate users and agents, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce rate limits and our terms, and protect the rights and safety of users and the public.
- To communicate with you about the Services, including announcements, security alerts, and support messages.
- To respond to your requests, questions, and feedback.
- To create aggregated or de-identified data, which we may use and share for our lawful business purposes, including analyzing and improving the Services.
- To train, fine-tune, and evaluate models we run or develop. We do not sell your content, and we do not share it with a third party to train that party's models except where you direct us to send a request to a model provider so it can answer.
- To comply with applicable laws, lawful requests, and legal process.

## 3. How We Share Information

## 4. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, while we have a business need, or as required by law (for example, for tax, accounting, billing, or legal purposes), whichever is longer. Usage receipts and certain publicly projected records may be retained as part of the verifiable record of activity.

**Coder chats saved to your account.** Coder saves chats only on your computer unless you turn on saving them to your account (it is off by default). When it is on, we keep each chat's title, its messages, and your computer's name, visible only to you when you are signed in. Coder leaves out messages that look like they hold a password or key, and we refuse any that get through. A saved chat stays until you delete it, in Coder or on the website (deleting it in one deletes it in both), or until it goes unused for as long as we keep web chats. Turning saving off stops new uploads, and Coder can delete the chats it already saved.

## 5. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, preferences, security, and to understand how the Services are used. Most browsers let you remove or reject cookies through their settings; doing so may affect functionality such as staying signed in.

**Website analytics.** On openagents.com we measure use ourselves, without third-party analytics or tracking scripts and without cookies. The cookie exceptions are the cookie for affiliate and campaign links described in section 6, which is a separate feature set only when you follow such a link, and the playtest's session cookie described under Playtest research below. We record only aggregate hourly totals: which kind of page was opened, the name of the referring site, the type of device (phone, tablet, or computer), whether the visit came from a person, an automated agent, or a crawler, response status and speed, and a fixed list of actions (sending a chat, receiving an answer, clicking a suggestion, card, or download, copying the install command, and signing in). For this purpose we do not record IP addresses, account identifiers, the content you submit, full page or referrer addresses, or any identifier that distinguishes one visitor from another. We keep these totals for up to 13 months.

**Visits to the Grid homepage.** On the Grid homepage (openagents.com/home), we also record what each visit does, in order, so we can see where people get stuck. When you open the page, it makes a random number for your visit and keeps it in that browser tab only (not a cookie); it is gone when you close the tab. With that number we record a fixed list of steps: the page and the Grid loading (how long it took, and whether your browser drew it with WebGPU or WebGL), joining the Grid, walking to the terminal, entering the Pylon Bay, pointing at or opening a Pylon (only its kind, such as a Mac or a GPU machine), going through a portal and which place it led to, Game Lab steps, choosing a model, sending a message, whether an answer came and roughly how long it took, clicking Log in or Sign up, signing in, and roughly how long the page was open. We never record what you type, your account, your IP address, keys, or Pylon names with it, and we never connect the number to your account or to a cookie. We delete these records after 30 days.

**Playtest research.** On the pages of our game playtest (the homepage, the playtest and its tutorial, the survey, and the game's house and world pages), we record what each visit does in more detail, so we can see where players get stuck and fix it. This is separate from the totals above, and our own small script does it; no third party receives it. We record:

- each page you open and each move between pages, how long the page was open and in view, and how far you scrolled;
- each click: the name or id the page gives the button or link you clicked, its role, and up to 60 characters of its visible text;
- when you send a form, the names of its fields, never what you typed in them (we never record what you type anywhere);
- errors in our pages (the message and which of our script files it came from);
- the game's progress steps (for example, the game finished loading or a tutorial step was completed), and the survey being started and finished;
- the size of your screen and whether it is a touch screen, and the type of device, operating system, and browser (for example, "phone, iOS, Safari"), not the full browser description;
- the campaign link that brought you, if any (for example, "reddit, reddit-gamedev").

These records carry a random session number: the one the playtest keeps in a cookie named oa_playtest, or, before you start the playtest, one your browser tab keeps until you close it. If you are signed in, we keep your account's id with them. We never record your IP address with them. We keep them for 180 days, and only we see them.

You can turn this off in the playtest's introduction. When it is off, or when your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we keep only counts of which pages were opened and which kinds of event happened, with no session number, account, click text, or detail.

When you start the playtest from a campaign link, a second cookie, oa_playtest_from, keeps the name of the site or campaign that sent you (for example, "newsletter") for 7 days, so your survey can say how you found us.

**Playtest answers, the waitlist, and profiles.** The survey keeps your answers, your playtest's session number, your type of device and browser, whether you turned recording off, and, if you give them, your email and whether we may contact you; if you're signed in, it also keeps your account's id. Joining the waitlist keeps your email, the role you chose, the site that sent you, and the time. The profile keeps your role, the engines you use, your experience, what you want to make, how you heard about us, your email, whether we may contact you, and your account's id if you're signed in. We use these answers to improve OpenAgents and, if you agreed, to email you about the beta and future playtests. Only we see them. We keep them until you ask us to delete them, or for up to 24 months after the beta ends. Deleting your account deletes its profile. To have any of it deleted, email [chris@openagents.com](mailto:chris@openagents.com) from the address you gave us.

Our hosting provider's request logs, which we use to operate and secure the Services, include IP addresses and are deleted after 30 days.

**Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.** When your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, our website analytics count only that a page was opened (the kind of page, its status, and its speed), and nothing else about your visit. The Grid homepage makes no visit number and records no steps, and playtest research keeps only counts. We also set no affiliate or campaign link cookie (section 6).

## 6. Affiliate and Campaign Links

Some links to openagents.com are campaign links we post ourselves (they start with openagents.com/go/), and some are referral links shared by people and partners who recommend us (they start with openagents.com/r/). When you follow one, we remember which link brought you, so we can see which posts bring people who sign up and credit the person who recommended us. This is separate from our website analytics in section 5, which stay cookieless.

**What we keep in your browser.** One first-party cookie named oa_ref, which only our website can read. It holds the link you followed (our campaign code, or the referral link), the series the link names, a random click number, and the time. It lasts 30 days, and following a newer link replaces it. We delete it when you log in or sign up.

**What we keep on our servers.** For each click: the random click number, the link's campaign code (for a referral link, a short code made from the link instead of the link itself), the series, the time, the page it led to, and whether the click came from a person, an automated agent, or a crawler. We do not record your IP address, your device or browser, or your account for this. We keep click records for up to 13 months. If you create an account while the cookie is set, we keep with your account which link brought you and its click number; this stays with your account like its other sign-up details. If you later pay for a plan, we record that sale against the same link, so we can see which links lead to purchases and, under the terms we publish for affiliates, pay the people who share referral links a commission.

**Who sees it.** We do. A person who shares a referral link may see totals for their own link (clicks, sign-ups, and sales), never who you are.

**Your choices.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, or we can't tell where you are, we ask before setting the cookie, and set nothing if you say no. When your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we set no cookie and record only that a link was followed. Automated agents and crawlers never get the cookie and are never credited. You can delete the cookie in your browser at any time.
