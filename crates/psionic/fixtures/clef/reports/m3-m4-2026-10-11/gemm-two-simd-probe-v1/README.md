Do not integrate this candidate. The one approved run stopped at its unchanged 60-second deadline, before completing the frozen timing plan. No retry, larger cap, or other group-count variant was attempted.

All 14 synthetic and five real-weight correctness checks passed with zero bit differences. Every real gate, up, half activation, and down output matched the four-group baseline exactly; independent f64 checks also passed. The run completed 149 of 240 timed output checks, all bitwise equal, before stopping during the final 1024-token round. It completed all five 512-token rounds. No 2048-token timing ran. These partial measurements do not replace the missing full-suite gate. Shared-machine load, cache effects, and temporal drift remain limitations.
