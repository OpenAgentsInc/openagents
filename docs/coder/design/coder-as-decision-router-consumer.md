# Coder as a decision service consumer

Status: target product and host contract. Coder is a coding specialization of
[general agent infrastructure](../../agents/README.md). It consumes typed
decision services as one part of a system that also uses deterministic tools,
generation, and bounded compositions.

The product promise is useful work with understandable evidence, cost, and
control. An increased number of model calls or a confident display does not
establish that result. Each uncertain operation needs a semantic contract,
an appropriate implementation, and workload evaluation.

## What the decision service supplies

A decision service accepts state and typed questions and returns typed
judgments with model identity, outcomes, usage, and attributable receipts.
Noul, Choice, and Score support probability, categorical, and rubric decisions.
They do not generate arbitrary text, authorize effects, or prove calibration.

A consumer handles refusal, unknown state, budget limits, and cancellation
explicitly. It validates output shape and the answer space before a policy
consumes the result. It records the actual state, questions, target, and
consuming policy. Explicit structured requests need no redundant semantic
classification when mechanical rules already determine the next action.

## Semantic AI operations and optimization

Define operations by meaning: select sufficient evidence, identify an eligible
workflow, propose a patch, or produce a cited explanation. Bind each semantic
AI signature to an exact supported implementation. Its realization can use
a decision function, generation operation, retrieval strategy, or program.

The [optimization architecture](../../optimization/architecture.md) keeps
model-specific wording, demonstrations, inference strategy, and authorized
internal decomposition replaceable. DSPy/GEPA can search those choices;
Gym and domain evaluators measure candidates. Host authority, disclosure,
budgets, effect checks, and final acceptance remain protected.

[NIP-OPT](../../../nips/openagents/NIP-OPT.md) defines signatures,
implementations, studies, materialization, and trials.
[The proposed issues](../../optimization/proposed-issues.md) specify full
integration without opening issues or running experiments.

## One host execution path

Interactive and automated callers share admission, state, execution, and
receipt semantics. Clients render state and user decisions; they do not
maintain a second scheduler or permission policy.

Admit each model recipient and operation independently. Every model call,
including reflection and judging, consumes an attributed reservation. A
failed parse, refused answer, unknown cost, and completed task are different
outcomes. No fallback silently widens authority or discloses additional data.

## Repository evidence selection

Capture source versions, diagnostics, search results, and observations once
under explicit scope. Preserve originals and mark incomplete captures.
Representations retain their derivation and support bounded expansion.

A context request identifies the task, recipient, needed evidence, and limits.
Mandatory instructions are selected by authority and scope, not by relevance
scores. Retrieval and semantic selection have separate coverage measurements.
The resulting manifest records exactly what each operation saw and omitted.

Evaluate context policies on downstream task quality, harmful omissions,
expansion, latency, and total cost. Fewer tokens alone do not establish a
better coding agent.

## Programs as application structure

A program composes typed operations under exact dependency pins and shared
bounds. Selection proposes a workflow or `none`; it neither installs a
component nor authorizes execution. A direct structured request can resolve
a workflow without a model call.

Program inputs and predecessor outputs use bounded typed bindings. Resolve
the full graph and schemas before effects. A supported AI implementation
may change its internal inference graph while preserving the program's
declared task and protected control transitions.

### Program authority

Grants are independent host decisions. Every invocation narrows parent scope
and reservations. Capability presence does not authorize use; semantic
confidence does not override denied disclosure or a known conflict.

### Work intake

Identify user intent, required artifacts, source scope, acceptance, and
unresolved questions. Quoted commands and descriptions of programs must not
activate work. Requests outside admitted scope are refused or clarified.

### Independence and scheduling

Use explicit dependencies, known resource conflicts, and fenced claims.
Semantic judgments can propose independence where mechanics cannot settle it,
but they cannot waive known dependencies. Preparation and assimilation count
against the full task budget.

### Independent completion

Verify produced artifacts against protected acceptance criteria and exact
source state. Successful children do not prove parent completion. Applying
or publishing an artifact is a separately admitted effect with fresh
preconditions.

### Durable program runs

Persist admission and dispatch intent before effects. Recover through exact
pins and attributable records. Preserve unknown effects and unsettled spend
until reconciliation. Cancellation acknowledgment is not proof of remote stop.

### Bounded program composition

Child programs share parent reservations and declared effect limits. Resolve
cycles, schemas, depth, and fan-out before dispatch. Bounded repair is explicit
work; a generated plan cannot introduce hidden recursive execution.

### Portable program registry

Use qualified component identities, immutable dependencies, inert discovery,
and scoped installation. Public package signatures provide provenance.
Local bindings and operator grants determine what actually runs.

## Receipts and replay

Record task, implementation, input/context, policy, model, tool, attempt,
usage, outcome, verification, and integration identities. Optimization adds
study/candidate/trial and actual materialization references.

Replay reconstructs recorded evidence without effects. Re-execution is a new
admitted attempt. Retention loss remains visible and cannot become proof
that an unknown operation did nothing.

## Terminal and automated experience

Show the current objective, selected evidence, active implementation, required
approval, budget, and unresolved outcomes. Explain unavailable operations and
stale findings. Give advanced readers access to exact provenance without
putting compiler internals into ordinary task flows.

Present candidate selection, confirmation, and adoption separately. An
optimizer's best candidate can still fail final evaluation or remain ineligible
for deployment.

## Delivery sequence and acceptance

Follow the [roadmap](typesafe-agent-roadmap.md): useful evidence-based answers,
a native coding loop, economical context/routing, parallel/background work,
and durable reusable components. Build the optimization lifecycle alongside
those contracts, then expand its search surfaces as enforcement and evidence
justify.

Acceptance requires complete-task comparisons, explicit uncertainty, protected
confirmation, and truthful no-win outcomes. A non-coding fixture verifies that
shared contracts do not depend on repositories or terminal state. Paid
experiments, data export, package publication, and deployment have separately
admitted scope.
