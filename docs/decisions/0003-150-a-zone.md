# 0003. 150 concurrent players per zone shard

Date: 2026-11-11
Status: Accepted
Source: [Everglade and Aethyr against AAA MMORPG practice](../audits/2026-11-11-everglade-aethyr-aaa-audit.md), section G4; [everglade/ARCHITECTURE.md](../../everglade/ARCHITECTURE.md)

## Context

`EG_SEATS :: 16` (`coax/web/room/room-everglade.coax`) capped each room at 16 players and broadcast every seat to every connected client at the room tick rate over JSON/WebSocket frames. Sixteen seats is party scale rather than MMORPG town or world-boss scale, while naive all-to-all replication saturates bandwidth and tick time as player and NPC counts grow.

## Decision

Target **150 concurrent players (plus active NPCs) per authoritative zone shard** at a steady 20 Hz server tick:

- Spatial interest management (`metaverse-engine/net/grid.coax`) filters replication by cell neighborhood and priority budget (`metaverse-engine/net/budget.coax`) instead of all-to-all broadcast.
- Hot-path state snapshots and input commands use compact binary frames over WebTransport (`coax/web/room/wt.c`) with WebSocket fallback.
- Overflow beyond a shard's soft cap routes arriving players to an additional shard of the same zone via `/api/verse/rooms` (`coax/web/api/rooms.c`), keeping party members together.

## Consequences

- Shard memory, interest-grid cells, and outbound byte budgets are sized and soak-tested against 150 players + NPCs with tick p99 under 50 ms.
- Clients never assume every player in the zone is in their local entity table; distant actors enter and leave through interest-set deltas.
