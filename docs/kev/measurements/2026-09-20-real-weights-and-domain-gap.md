# Two checkpoints with real weights: the gated suite, and our suite against public labels

Three issues had boxes that waited on Kev weights being present on the
machine that ran them.
[openagents#9426](https://github.com/OpenAgentsInc/openagents/issues/9426)
shipped a weights-gated conformance suite that had only ever skipped;
[openagents#9380](https://github.com/OpenAgentsInc/openagents/issues/9380)
asked whether the adapters' scores on our own labelled suite hold on
labelled data nobody here wrote. This record is both, for the two
checkpoints that fit on this machine: `kev-0.5b` and `kev-0.6b`. The latency
half of the same session is
[`../../gym/measurements/2026-09-20-kev-quiet-latency.md`](../../gym/measurements/2026-09-20-kev-quiet-latency.md).

The Lev side of #9380 was not attempted. Lev answers through Apple's
on-device model and this machine is not Apple hardware. Nothing here bears
on `docs/decision-models/lev/disposition.md` except by implication, and the
implication is stated at the end rather than written into that file.

## The machine

CPU only, fp32, the `kev-serve` default and the precision the fixtures pin.

```text
cpu: INTEL(R) XEON(R) PLATINUM 8559C, 8 cores, KVM guest
mem: 31 GiB
kernel: 5.15.200 (Linux)
toolchain: rust-toolchain.toml as pinned; tree at 04c4b4e6b
```

Weights came from `./scripts/fetch-kev-artifacts.sh kev-0.5b kev-0.6b`,
which reported every pinned adapter digest matching for both, into
`~/repos/kev-artifacts/` and not into git. `kev-0.6b` publishes no
`eval.json`; the script now fetches that file only when the fixture manifest
lists it, which is the one code change this session made. `kev-4b`'s base
fetch stalled for an hour at 5.4 GB and was stopped; `kev-8b` was not
attempted.

## The gated suite, with weights present

The `kev` test suite under `--features serve` has six conformance tests, five
encoding tests, and four serving tests that read weights and skip with
`skipping, no artifacts` when none are present. #9426's own round trip
recorded exactly that skip. With `KEV_VARIANT` naming a fetched checkpoint:

```text
KEV_VARIANT=kev-0.5b KEV_ARTIFACT_DIR=$A/kev-0.5b KEV_BASE_DIR=$A/qwen2.5-0.5b \
    cargo test -p kev --features serve --release
KEV_VARIANT=kev-0.6b KEV_ARTIFACT_DIR=$A/kev-0.6b KEV_BASE_DIR=$A/qwen3-0.6b \
    cargo test -p kev --features serve --release
```

| Variant | `api` | `conformance` | `encode` | `refusals` | `serve` | Skipped | Failed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `kev-0.5b` | 5 | 6 (5.2 s) | 5 | 13 | 4 (2.8 s) | 0 | 0 |
| `kev-0.6b` | 5 | 6 (6.4 s) | 5 | 13 | 4 (3.5 s) | 0 | 0 |

Thirty-three tests per variant, none skipped. The conformance tests
compare the port's logits, pointer readout, and probabilities against the
golden fixtures generated by the Python reference at the tolerances the
fixtures pin; the serving tests start `kev-serve` on the real weights, ask
it over `POST /v1/systemone`, and check admission, the alias table, and the
refusal shapes end to end. The `KEV_VARIANT=kev-0.6b` run exercises the
Qwen3 backbone path, which the committed `kev-0.5b` fixtures alone cannot.

`kev-4b` and `kev-8b` have committed fixtures under
`crates/kev/fixtures/variants/` and still skip on this machine. Their gated
runs are open.

## Our suite against public labels

#9380's question, for the Kev side: the adapters were scored on
`support-v2-three-way`, whose items and labels this repository wrote. Do
those scores hold on labelled data we did not author?

`external-v1` is 200 items: 100 BoolQ validation items as a noul family and
100 MultiNLI validation-matched items as a three-way choice family, labels
the sets' own, question text the only authored part, 80 calibration, 80
development, 40 locked and unread.
[`../../decision-models/2026-09-20-instrument-validity.md`](../../decision-models/measurements/2026-09-20-instrument-validity.md)
built it and scored hosted Jev on it; this session added the two Kev doors
to the same store.

```text
gym eval --door kev-0.5b=http://127.0.0.1:8101 --suite crates/gym/suites/external-v1.json \
    --record crates/gym/results/external-v1.jsonl --fit --timeout 60
gym eval --door kev-0.6b=http://127.0.0.1:8102 --suite crates/gym/suites/external-v1.json \
    --record crates/gym/results/external-v1.jsonl --fit --timeout 60
```

One caveat decides how to read the result, and it is the reason the section
is not titled "out of domain". **BoolQ and MNLI are two of Kev's training
sources.** [`../model-cards.md`](../model-cards.md) lists both, and reports
`kev-0.5b` at 0.753 on a held-out BoolQ split and 0.747 on held-out MNLI.
So `external-v1` is out of domain for *our labels* — it is the number our
authorship cannot have fitted — and in distribution for *the adapter*. The
model card's own out-of-domain suite (`evals/transfer-v4`: QNLI, SciQ,
TweetEval, PAWS, MMLU, Emotion) is not a Gym suite here, so the transfer
gap the card reports for the preview family was not re-measured.

### The panel

Both suites read on their open partitions, 157 items and 160 items. The
support numbers are from the quiet store,
`crates/gym/results/support-v2-three-way-quiet.jsonl`, and are identical
item for item to the 2026-09-19 contended rows.

| Door | `support-v2-three-way` | `external-v1` | External minus support | BoolQ (80) | MNLI (80) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hosted Jev | 0.930 (146/157) | 0.856 (137/160) | -0.074 | 0.900 | 0.813 |
| `kev-0.5b` | 0.713 (112/157) | 0.669 (107/160) | -0.045 | 0.675 | 0.663 |
| `kev-0.6b` | 0.675 (106/157) | 0.781 (125/160) | **+0.106** | 0.763 | 0.800 |

Confident errors, wrong with a top probability at or above 0.9, on
`external-v1`: Jev 3, `kev-0.5b` 12, `kev-0.6b` 14.

### Reading it against the floor

The two-door accuracy floor is 0.056
([`../../lev/measurements/2026-09-19-seed-variance.md`](../../lev/measurements/2026-09-19-seed-variance.md)).
It was measured on `support-v2` and is used here for the external suite too,
which is a borrowing, stated as one.

- **`kev-0.5b` moves 0.045, under the floor.** Its score on public labels is
  its score on ours. This is also 0.08 below the card's held-out BoolQ and
  MNLI figures, which were measured on the card's own splits and templates,
  not on this suite's question wording.
- **`kev-0.6b` moves +0.106, two floors, the wrong way for a fitting
  story.** It is the weakest door on our labels and the stronger Kev door
  on public ones. Against `kev-0.5b` on `external-v1` the paired test gives
  28 discordant items to 10, exact *p* = 0.005, so the reversal is not
  noise. On `support-v2` the same pair is 12 to 18, *p* = 0.36, tied.
- **Jev drops 0.074, one floor and a third**, from a ceiling of 0.930
  toward the published crowd-label ceilings the instrument-validity record
  named. It still leads both Kev doors on both suites; the paired test
  against `kev-0.6b` on `external-v1` is 23 to 11, *p* = 0.058, which is
  the one Jev-Kev comparison in this repository that does not clear
  0.05.

What the panel says for #9380: **the small adapters' in-domain scores are
not an artefact of our labels.** One holds within the floor; the other
rises. Neither loses two floors going from labels we wrote to labels we did
not, which is what "fitted to the suite" would look like. What it does not
say: anything about transfer to tasks the adapter was not trained on. BoolQ
and MNLI are Kev's material. For that question the card's `transfer-v4`
numbers stand, unreproduced here.

The ordering between the two small checkpoints is suite-dependent, and this
is the second time the repository has found that. The model card says 0.6B
to 4B is the capacity step that pays out of domain; `2026-09-19` found 4B to
8B is the step that pays on `support-v2`. Now the two smallest doors swap
places between our labels and public ones. Name the suite with the
checkpoint.

### What it implies for the Lev disposition

`docs/decision-models/lev/disposition.md` ranks doors on `support-v2`
labels alone. The Kev side of #9380 says that for `kev-0.5b` those labels
were not flattering it, and for `kev-0.6b` they were understating it by two
floors. The Lev side is the unmeasured half: `lev-adapted@1` at 0.943 on
`support-v2` has no `external-v1` row, and the adapter it uses was fitted on
`support-v2`'s own calibration partition, which is the one door where a
fitting story is on the table. That row is the measurement the disposition
correction needs, it needs Apple hardware, and this record does not touch
the file.

## What stays open

- **`kev-4b` and `kev-8b` with weights**: the gated suite for both, and both
  on `external-v1`. The fetch did not complete here.
- **A transfer suite in the Gym**: `external-v1` measures our labels against
  public ones for the same tasks. A suite from sources the adapter was not
  trained on would measure transfer, and would be the number to set beside
  the card's.
- **Lev on `external-v1`**: Apple hardware.
