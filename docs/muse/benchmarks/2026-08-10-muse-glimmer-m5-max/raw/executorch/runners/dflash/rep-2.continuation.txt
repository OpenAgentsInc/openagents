 Paris. It is the most populous city in France and the most populous city proper in the European Union. The Paris Region had a population of 12,405,426 in January 2023, the largest in the European Union, while the population of the city proper was 2,175,601. Since the 17th century, Paris has been one of Europe's major centres of finance, diplomacy, commerce, fashion, science and arts.
