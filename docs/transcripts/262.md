**Sarah:** Hello again. Let's talk strategy.

In three years of R&D we explored a lot. We never had one stable product to
anchor it all.

So we take a page from the Cursor playbook. They forked VS Code. We fork zed.

Out of the box we get a fast, native, open-source editor — with code,
collaboration, and agents.

That foundation is where we add the layers that matter: verification,
markets, revenue-sharing, and multiplayer.

We've called this the last IDE you'll ever need. So we call this product
Omega.

We're excited to put this in your hands. We'll release the first builds in
the coming days.
