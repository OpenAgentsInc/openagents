# Episode 290: Coder v1, the Metaharness — the cut (v1)

The first full cut of the [Episode 290 script](290.md), for the owner's
review before the voiceover is recorded. It was made in Video Lab through the
web chat as the document `episode-290-metaharness` (v18; the take-5 document
`episode-290` is unchanged). Review render: `~/Downloads/episode-290-metaharness-v1.mp4`,
1080p, 4:37.

- **The whiteboard** keeps your own voice, with the music ducked under it and
  captions of what you say. Every whiteboard cut sits in a pause.
- **The new voiceover isn't recorded.** Every new line is on screen as a
  caption at 150 words a minute, so the film reads the way it will sound
  (marked `"vo": true` in the cut). The Bella voiceover comes after your
  notes.
- **No pauses:** every animation plays straight through and every shot ends
  as its move does, on the 120 BPM beat grid. The Lab refuses a shot that
  holds a still frame or plays a still stretch of 0.4 s or more, and the
  final render was scanned for still stretches.
- **Transitions:** 0.5 s crossfades between animations. Where one animation
  opens on the frame the one before it ends on, the crossfade reads as one
  continuous camera move (routing beat 1 into the metaharness, routing beat 2
  into the savings). Hard cuts are kept only where the picture matches across
  them (the Shift+Tab highlight to the real Shift+Tab, the delegation report
  to the live agent tray).
