**How it was made:**
- **The whiteboard** keeps your own voice, with the music ducked under it and
  captions of what you say. Every whiteboard cut sits in a pause.
- **New lines that aren't recorded yet** are captions at 150 words a minute
  (`lab_lines`, marked `"vo": true` in the cut).
- **No pauses:** every animation plays straight through and every shot ends
  as its move does, on the 120 BPM beat grid. The Lab refuses a shot that
  holds a still frame or plays a still stretch of 0.4 s or more, and each
  final render is scanned for still stretches.
- **Transitions:** 0.5 s crossfades between animations. Where one animation
  opens on the frame the one before it ends on, the crossfade reads as one
  continuous camera move (routing beat 1 into the metaharness, routing beat 2
  into the savings). Hard cuts are kept only where the picture matches across
  them (the Shift+Tab highlight to the real Shift+Tab, the delegation report
  to the live agent tray).
