**November 3, 2026**

## The Penultimate Frontier

We'll go to space. We'll debate Trek or Wars, Earth or Mars or belt or outer planets.

First we'll care for humans on Earth.

The old world's power structures languish in decay. A bright new world struggles to be born. We are its midwives.

Millions of people now fall out of the old system with nowhere to go. Millions more, younger generations foremost, now realize there were never seats for them at those old tables.

Something new is needed. But what?

First consider what we have.

We have numbers. Call it the people against the elites or the ninety-nine percent against the one: any way you count it, we have the silent majority.

And nearly all of us wield this century's greatest tool — which if needed could become a weapon in our defense.

The computer. Whether in your pocket or on your desktop, we together have the computing power — which today equals the real power — to create or conquer nations.

But not when we and our devices are isolated, disconnected, used to argue with each other, our attention farmed for engagement to prop up yesterday's winners.

We must refocus our attention and repurpose our computing power.

We can meet together in that space created when our computers connect — called cyberspace.

Its forefathers met on bulletin boards. We will meet in virtual worlds — on the penultimate frontier.

One author named it the Oasis. Today we name it the Verse.

We hope to meet you there.
