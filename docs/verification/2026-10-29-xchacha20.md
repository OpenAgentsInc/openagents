# XChaCha20 stream primitive, initial evidence

This is raw XChaCha20, not authenticated encryption or a complete NIP-49
backup. It must not be used to protect secrets without authentication, nonce
management, proven erasure, and target review. Its internal ABI requires
valid views and a truthful alias observation. No target-side timing or
production-secret approval is claimed.

The [XChaCha Internet-Draft](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-irtf-cfrg-xchacha-03#appendix-A.3.2.1)
keystream vector passes, as do all 399 mutants in the loaded Coax graph.
`coax/tests/nostr/xchacha20.py` derives subkeys with an independent round-state
model and compares 513 inputs with OpenSSL's ChaCha20 per checked and release
native and Wasm profile. Native boundary paths passed ASan/UBSan; both profiles
compile for arm64 iOS Simulator. iOS execution and physical devices are not
covered.

and source `086b7ab5f9c89ffd6c2c44ab8649d5961891c9278fb21c8b50cfb9445419bafd`.

defect within this scope; it did not approve erasure, timing or production use.
