# Bitcoin chapter 63 qualification, 2026-11-04

`kvwriteback.coax` coalesces a checked immutable base and a bounded private
delta. Actual base presence decides whether an edit can disappear. Fresh
create/spend cancels; spending, re-adding and spending an existing base coin
retains its deletion. Empty values stay distinct from absence. Caller-owned
scratch and tagged-record workspaces make allocation bounds explicit.

`coinscache.coax` accepts complete successful validated transition bodies,
binds the durable cursor, refuses light/foreign profiles and checks its exact
resident cache budget. The cache is private; it cannot grant consensus validity
or publish a newer active index. A flush freezes edits, coalesces the complete
coins/inverses/undo/receipt/tip transition and requires durable acknowledgement
of its generation and tip. A known pre-append abort retains dirty work. An
ambiguous write requires closing and checked recovery.
