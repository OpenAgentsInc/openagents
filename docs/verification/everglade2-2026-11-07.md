# Everglade II verification, 2026-11-07

This note checks Everglade II (`/worlds/everglade2`, issue #13023) against the issue's done
conditions. The files are in `everglade2-2026-11-07/`.

All captures and measurements ran in headless Google Chrome 157 on an Apple silicon Mac, on the
hardware Metal adapter (`apple`, `metal-3`), at 1920 by 1080 and a device pixel ratio of 1. A
windowed real-Chrome pass has not been done yet.

## The walk

`walk-1080p.mp4` is 54 seconds at 30 frames a second. It joins three 18-second runs made with
`?walk`, which steers your character down the path with the follow camera behind:

| Part | Start | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The glade (`?walk`) | Character Create's glade, then the path into the woods |
| 2 | 200 m (`?at=200&walk`) | Dense woods crowding the path, with light coming through |
| 3 | 410 m (`?at=410&walk`) | The woods opening into the clearing, where the walk stops |

The runs were filmed at a run. `?walk` now walks, as the keys do with Shift held.

## Looking round

Each image holds four views, a quarter turn of the camera apart:

- `look-at-0.jpg`: the glade, with the woods closing round it on every side.
- `look-at-200.jpg`: the middle of the path.
- `look-at-450.jpg`: the clearing, ringed by woods.

## Frame rate

`bun coax/web/frametime.ts URL 1920x1080 1 8 --vsync`, on a quiet machine (1-minute load
under 5):

| Page | GPU time a frame (p50) | Frame interval p50 | Frame interval p95 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/worlds/everglade2` (the glade) | 7.8 to 8.3 ms | 16.67 ms | 16.67 ms |
| `?at=200` | 6.6 ms | 16.67 ms | 16.67 ms |
| `?at=200&walk` | 6.5 ms | 16.67 ms | 16.67 ms |
| `?at=450` (the clearing) | 6.0 ms | 16.67 ms | 16.67 ms |

Under load from other work on the machine (1-minute load near 15), the glade measured 22 to
25 ms a frame. Measure on a quiet machine.

## Build time

The page module builds in 1 to 4 seconds with a warm cache (17 seconds cold), the shader module
in seconds, and the pack in about 17 seconds.
