its native adapter. It derives gradients by central differences of the wide
value equations, before final narrowing. Bounds are frozen at
`abs(got-reference)/(1+abs(reference)) <= 2e-6` for outputs and `3e-5` for VJPs.

The native harness covers 38 cases spanning all 27 operators in three layouts:
contiguous, reversed last axis where applicable, and transposed rank-two

It checks shape/dtype inference, exact selected indices, read-only input and

## Scope

All values/masks are f32; ordering auxiliaries and indexed labels are i64.
Rank 0–4 views and at most 1,048,576 elements/work units are admitted. Matrix
multiplication is rank two; convolution is NCHW/OIHW valid cross-correlation,
stride one/groups one; pooling is valid NCHW; interpolation uses the declared
floor/half-pixel mapping; losses are mean MSE and rank-two class cross entropy.
The manifest lists every implemented variant instead of implying wider support.

Arithmetic uses f64 products/ordered compensated reductions and checked f32
narrowing, with FMA/fast-math disabled. The direct analytical VJP contracts
input/output derivative pairs under a separate work bound and can refuse a
forward-admitted request. It is a small CPU correctness reference, not a claim
of large-model backward throughput or a complete general autograd engine.

Metadata/input/alias refusals leave all spans untouched. Computed arithmetic
failure may dirty staging but leaves all public value/index/gradient outputs
unchanged and reports zero counts. Caller-owned pointers/extents, alignment,
lifetime, immutable inputs, exclusive access and fresh/unpublished result spans
remain required. To replace a registered tensor, results pass through chapter
2a's versioned scatter boundary; arbitrary external writes and forged records
are outside the contract. Functional VJPs require current inputs; a saved graph
must check its saved-version tokens first.
