## Independent oracle and error scope

[ops_oracle.py](../../psionic/tests/ops_oracle.py) uses Python's standard-library
Decimal at precision 120, exact binary32 input encodings and integer LCG seed
734. The reference computes high-range direct exponentials for softmax and
`E[x*x] - E[x]^2` for LayerNorm, rather than duplicating the production max
subtraction / centered-variance passes. It never reads implementation output.
References are serialized to 80 decimal places and then rounded to the native
harness's `long double` for comparison; that does not imply 120-digit native
arithmetic or that `long double` is wider than `double` on this host.

For the bounded softmax/norm fixtures, several binary32 rounding units plus
a fixed absolute allowance cover narrowing and target scalar math. The input
normalization magnitudes are at most four, widths at most seven, and epsilon
is the actual f32 encoding of `1e-5`. These are independently chosen fixture
bounds, not universal libm accuracy bounds. The chapter states a separate
conditioning-aware conservative reduction bound under the IEEE profile.

Generated fixture-header SHA-256: `271b392a426255ce1be49afdea18d250b6023e661905e5f75fc2f8de6c168a6b`.
The generator is deterministic; it was regenerated and compared byte-for-byte.
The header is local test tooling and is not a runtime or downloaded artifact.

## Native-only defect controls

Two temporary copies of our C address adapter were compiled against the real
library and complete native harness:

- Measure integer-index spans with `sizeof(float)` instead of `sizeof(int64_t)`.
- Measure mask spans with `sizeof(float)` instead of `sizeof(uint8_t)`.

Both variants compiled, then failed native assertions (signal 6). The correctly
measured adapter passes. Controls were temporary and are not installed or
committed. They supplement the Coax mutation runs; arbitrary native defects
and generated iteration scaffolding are not exhaustively mutated. Actual
non-finite float validation remains a native check because the evaluator
refuses constructing such floats at compile time.

## Supported scope and limits

`CPU-f32-wide-v1`: contiguous row/matrix binary32 inputs/outputs, explicit
binary64 products and compensated reductions, increasing inner indices and
one narrowing per output; no FMA/reassociation. Each logical span is at most
1,048,576 elements. Matmul work also admits at most 1,048,576 multiply-adds.
Both float operands must be finite, even at masked/unselected positions.
Masks are 0/1 bytes with at least one allowed entry per row; indices have
explicit bounds. Normalizations are population-statistic row cores without
learned affine gain/bias. Epsilon is positive and finite inside the root.

All validation completes before staging. Success publishes exactly the planned
output prefix; unused writable capacity and read-only inputs stay unchanged.
Pre-execution refusal preserves scratch too. A computed out-of-range result
returns `arithmetic`, leaves destination unchanged and may leave a partial
scratch prefix. Underflow to a subnormal/zero is allowed. Native callers still
own truthful aligned allocations, view extents, lifetime, a truthful overlap
observation when bypassing the wrapper, and exclusive access during calls.
Address comparisons cannot establish arbitrary pointer ownership.

M1 is implemented for this teaching profile. Learned affine layers, arbitrary
axis/broadcast/strided/batched operations, bigger checkpoint resource profiles,
autodiff, model import, alternate devices and timing/quality claims remain
separate work. No performance benchmark, general memory-safety proof, owner
action, external model download or paid hardware was involved.
