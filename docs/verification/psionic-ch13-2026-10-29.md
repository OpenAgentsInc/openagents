# Chapter 13: qualification verification, 2026-10-29

Chapter 13 adds a qualification library and applies it to chapter 10's
checked-in Elephant evidence. No model files, downloads or services were used
for the results below. The checks ran on CoderOS (Linux x86_64, 28 cores) with
coax0 0.22.0 built from `origin/main` at `ba89b09165` (rebased onto
later `origin/main` before the final book and gate checks), GCC 15.2.0 and Python 3.15.0rc1 for
the Unicode 17.0 text fixtures.

## What was built

- [`psionic/lib/qualify.coax`](../../psionic/lib/qualify.coax), `#authority`:
  comparison policies (mode, unit, per-value tolerance, total variation,
  top-k agreement with near-tie margin and regret, profiles and the data
  set's identity) with a SHA-256 identity over a fixed 172-byte encoding;
  comparison of candidate and reference values; build-bound records in which
  every absent field is unknown; the verdict order (known mismatch fails,
  comparison failure fails, then unknown, then agrees or qualified); the exit
  status; and summary checking by recomputation.
- [`psionic/lib/qualify-elephant.coax`](../../psionic/lib/qualify-elephant.coax),
  `#authority`: the three chapter 10 policies with pinned digests, the
  record chapter 10 supports, and the evidence binding.
- [`psionic/lib/qualify-report.coax`](../../psionic/lib/qualify-report.coax)
  and [`psionic/lib/qualify-gate.coax`](../../psionic/lib/qualify-gate.coax):
  printing only, and the gate whose return value is the exit status.
- [`psionic/tests/qualify_oracle.py`](../../psionic/tests/qualify_oracle.py)
  (tooling): independent digest vectors, the generated evidence fixture and a
  check that every pinned digest in `qualify-elephant.coax` equals an
  independent computation.
- [`psionic/tests/qualify.sh`](../../psionic/tests/qualify.sh) with the
  native host [`psionic/tests/qualify.c`](../../psionic/tests/qualify.c).

All values are within their policies. Each verdict is unknown because chapter
10 recorded no digest of the sources that produced its candidate files and no
arithmetic profile for that build; the gate exits with status 2. The
thresholds are chapter 10's, chosen after its data was read, so the request
is recorded as a development set. A complete record would give `agrees`
(status 0), and a claim of qualification on that set exits with status 3.

The evidence binding: the layer-0 values are embedded as their exact f32 bits
and Coax recomputes the SHA-256 of both files (`bb24840a…` and `96355941…`)
before comparing. The decision text and request token file are embedded as
text and their SHA-256 (`1cf6754f…`, `3c72fddd…`) is recomputed in Coax. The
decimal values are copied verbatim from that text and from
`transformers-reference.json` by the tooling script, which checks that
transcription. The model identity `e8da2029…f0ed71b` is the SHA-256 of the
`sha256sum` listing of the four pinned Elephant files.

Chapter 10's numbers were rechecked and are unchanged: the layer-0 maximum
difference (0.000001431), the decision probabilities and slot mass, and the
claim that the three decision differences are below 0.0000002 (the largest is
1.89e-7) all reproduce. One detail chapter 10 does not state: the reference
probabilities are float32 values and sum to 0.9999999403953552, so the total
variation (2.98e-8) is smaller than the largest single difference.

## Limits

The policy digest makes a change to a policy visible; it does not prove when
the policy was written and is not a signature. The library compares the
values a caller supplies and cannot establish that they came from the device
and profile a record names; that remains the duty of the harness that runs
the model. No confirmation set exists yet, so nothing here is qualified.
`psionic/check.sh` does not yet call `psionic/tests/qualify.sh`; it runs the
chapter's example through the book check. Wiring the script into the
combined check is left to a change that owns that file. Checks inside the
Coax evaluator are limited to 20 million steps each, so the complete layer-0
qualification (two 16 KiB file digests and two 32 KiB value digests) runs
natively in the example and the gate; the checks pin each digest separately
and qualify layer 0 without its value digests.
