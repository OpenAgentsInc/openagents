# Chapter 215: record readings, join outcomes, watch drift, 2026-10-29

Verified on macOS 26.4 arm64 (Apple M5 Max) with Apple clang 21.0.0, Python
3.15.1 and coax0 0.24.0. No model weights, GPU, network or upstream code were
used.

`psionic/lib/drift.coax` implements profile `cpu-f64-drift-v1`:
- binary64, chapter 4's compensated accumulator for every sum;
- a record per decision with no content: model, question set, map and policy
  versions, raw and calibrated probability, threshold, eligible actions,
  action, selection probability, outcome status, arrival, label and cost;
- outcomes joined once, at most half a window late, one in ten lost;
- a policy acting yes from a threshold, exploring the other action with
  probability 0.1 except within 0.1 of 0 or 1;
- windows of 50 to 1,000 decisions, flagged when fewer than 2 of 200 label
  draws from the window's own probabilities reach its calibration error;
- refits every four windows through chapter 213's kinds and gate, against the
  map in use, from raw probabilities of outcomes already joined;
- inverse-propensity estimates of other thresholds, refused where the log
  never made an action eligible;
- the synthetic world `drift-sim-v1`.

## What mutation testing changed

The first run left 36 survivors, the second 8:
- **Percentile to count:** a sorted 99th percentile, where ties made `>` and
  `>=` the same, became a count of draws reaching the window, with checks
  for exactly 1 and 2 draws and for a perfect window.
- **Lost outcomes** are a floor instead of a comparison.
- **Refits** report every kind's gate results, follow a counter rather than a
  remainder, and are checked for a three-way tie, a refused fit, the
  base-rate map that stays, and raw rather than calibrated probabilities.
- **Waiting lists** store id + 1, so they need no initialization loop.
- **Calibration rows** are plain observed records, not decisions.
- **The watcher** became its own procedure over the log, which made small
  windows testable within the interpreter's step budget.

## Not claimed

- **Synthetic drift,** one shift at a known time, one run.
- **No judged sample** for lost outcomes; alerts trigger nothing.
- **Small windows are weak:** a shift of 3 in about 45 rows went unflagged
  at some seeds.
- **Process:** checks were written alongside the code, not run against stubs
  first.
