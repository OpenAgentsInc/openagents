## What executes

Coax prepares genuine raw SDK cameras and physical state, tokenizes the actual
book/caddy instruction, executes the complete pinned original GR00T network on
Metal, decodes sixteen seven-coordinate commands and admits eight for execution.
A Coax supervisor owns epoch/request/tick admission. A Coax F64 OSC controller
owns goal updates, mass inverse, task pseudoinverses, nullspace posture, damping,
bias compensation, persistent gripper targets and actuator clipping.

C owns SDK/file/allocation/Metal effects. MuJoCo 3.3.1 advances one 2 ms step per
receipt. Every logical control tick consumes exactly 25 such steps. Inference
pauses simulation; 20 Hz is a logical rate, not a real-time claim. Weights and
six shader modules remain resident across requests and scene resets within a
slice. Runtime does not execute Python, a vendor NN engine or cloned scripts.

Inputs are authenticated immutable original files and exact experiment inputs;
shape metadata cannot authenticate replacement payloads. The original 7 GB
shards and tokenizer were already authenticated in chapter 190; unchanged files
were not rehashed or rerun through CPU whole-model checks.
