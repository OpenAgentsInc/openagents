# Chapter 237: a suite is three digests, 2026-10-29

Verified on macOS 26.4 arm64 (Apple M5 Max) with Apple clang 21.0.0, Python
3.15.1 and coax0 0.24.0. No model weights, GPU, network or upstream code were
used. Elephant's per-item results come from chapter 16's recorded run; nothing
was rerun.

`psionic/lib/suite.coax` implements profile `sha256-suite-v1`:
- SHA-256 (Coax's `sha256.coax`) over a fixed encoding: little-endian u64s,
  float bit patterns, texts as a length and bytes, and one tag per digest;
- an items digest over every item field, task, partition and the mask of
  locked partitions; a question digest over the questions only; a gate digest
  over every gate setting;
- up to 64 items, 64 partitions and 1,024 tasks; ids up to 64 bytes, states
  512, options 64, questions 256; limits from 0 to 10³⁰⁰;
- an append-only store: each receipt takes the next sequence number and the
  previous receipt's digest, and a locked partition of one items digest can be
  read once;
- comparisons only over the same items (and mask), gate and partition, in
  that order; questions may differ and are reported.

## What mutation testing changed

The first run left 27 survivors, each fixed in the checks:
- the receipt digest of a small store is pinned to hashlib;
- the hex and digest-equality helpers are tested on mismatches in the first
  and last nibble and byte;
- bounds at each limit, on both sides;
- a locked partition whose first receipt is the first in the store, and an
  open partition above the locked one.

## Limits

- No signatures: the chain shows a change, not who made it.
- One store held by the caller.
- Locked means once per items digest; an edited suite can be read again.
- Verdicts are not stored in receipts.
