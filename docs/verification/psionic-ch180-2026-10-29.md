# Chapter 239: gates that can say unverifiable, 2026-10-29

Verified on macOS 26.4 arm64 (Apple M5 Max) with Apple clang 21.0.0, Python
3.15.1 and coax0 0.24.0. No model weights, GPU, network or upstream code were
used. Elephant's results come from chapter 16's recorded run; nothing was
rerun.

`psionic/lib/verdict.coax` implements profile `cpu-f64-verdict-v1`:
- binary64, chapter 4's compensated accumulator for every sum;
- verdicts passed, failed or unverifiable, each with a reason; a result nobody
  set is unverifiable with reason none;
- clear meaning at most 1 replicate in 40 on the other side of a line;
- a decision gate: headroom first (chapter 238, against the floor, or 0 with
  no floor), then a floor of at least two blocks, then at least 10 tasks,
  then a task bootstrap against the floor;
- a probability gate for yes-or-no probabilities: higher log loss fails, then
  more confident errors (top label at least 0.9 and wrong) fail, then lower
  log loss passes; calibration error and accuracy are reported only;
- an evaluation gate: a failure stays, cost or seconds over a written limit
  fail, an unmeasured cost, time or limit makes the result unverifiable, and
  otherwise the quality verdict stands.

The native harness compiles `psionic/tests/verdict-native.coax`, which loads
the gates and chapter 214's thresholds; the gates themselves do not load
chapter 214.

## What mutation testing changed

The first run left nine survivors:
- **Zero values:** verdicts start with unverifiable and reasons with none, so
  removing the assignment of a pass is caught.
- **A check helper** is tested on mismatched verdicts and reasons.
- **Bounds and edges:** a single item, 10,000 replicates, an invalid first
  item, a probability of exactly 0.5 and a top label of exactly 0.9.
- **A redundant assignment** in a test fixture was removed.

## Limits

- No receipts or suite digests (chapter 237).
- The 1-in-40 line, 10 tasks and 0.9 are fixed constants.
- Yes-or-no probabilities only in the probability gate.
- No correction for comparing many candidates against one baseline.
