# Chapter 241: fit settings on recorded answers, 2026-10-29

Verified on macOS 26.4 arm64 (Apple M5 Max) with Apple clang 21.0.0, Python
3.15.1 and coax0 0.24.0. No model weights, GPU, network or upstream code were
used, and no model was called: the recorded answers are simulated.

`psionic/lib/settings.coax` implements profile `cpu-f64-settings-v1`:
- stall-sim-v1: runs of ten steps on chapter 103's SplitMix64 counters, each
  step with a recorded probability, a repeat count, quiet seconds and whether
  it was stalled;
- a rule of three settings: flag when the probability plus the weight times
  repeats over 8 reaches the threshold, or when the quiet seconds reach the
  cut point;
- written costs, counted as integers and priced at the end: 4 per missed
  stall, 1 per false alarm;
- candidates at the midpoints between the points where a decision changes,
  plus one value past each end; the weight held within [0, 4];
- a coordinate search (weight, threshold, cut point) that keeps a move only
  when strictly cheaper, and, with cross-validation, only when refitting
  without each fold of runs and pricing the fold is strictly cheaper in total
  than leaving the setting alone.

The native harness compiles `psionic/tests/settings-native.coax`, which loads
the settings and chapter 239's gates; the settings library does not load the
gates.

## What mutation testing changed

The first run left 40 survivors:
- **Two folds are symmetric:** swapping training and held-out runs gives the
  same total, so a three-fold fit is pinned, with a fold total exactly equal
  to the current cost.
- **Continuous comparisons:** a stall drawn by comparing two uniforms can't
  be tested at equality; it is now drawn by truncating a sum. Candidate
  filters became one comparison that a step can meet exactly.
- **Redundant guards** (an empty row set, a fold holding every row, repeats
  above zero) were removed where a later check already refused the case.
- **Held-out folds** are priced from the whole set's counts less the
  training runs', with no buffer of their own.
- **Check helpers** are tested on a mismatch in every field.

## Limits

- One rule of three settings; another layer needs its own rule.
- Coordinate search only: moves that pay only together are not found.
- Simulated records; real ones come from chapter 215's log and chapter 245.
