**What the example shows**
- The full loop's cost per accepted result falls from 2.2349 to 1.8131: a gain of 0.4218, with a standard error of 0.0682.
- Removing the proposer or the grader costs more than twice the standard error.
- Removing the reflector costs 0.0658 ± 0.0495. That is within noise, so the ledger flags its 32 units a run for removal.
- In the barren world, the loop adopts nothing and is reported `flat`, after spending 1,632 units a run.
