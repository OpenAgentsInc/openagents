# Chapter 247: an agent optimizes a Psionic kernel, 2026-10-30

**Environment:**
- **Mac:** macOS 26.4 arm64 (Apple M5 Max), coax0 0.24.0 built from this tree, Python 3.15.1.
- **L4:** the VM `psionic-l4-elephant` (`g2-standard-4` spot, us-central1-a), reached over IAP with the automation service account.
  - NVIDIA L4 (compute 8.9, 58 SMs), driver 13020.
  - NVRTC 13.2 from the PyPI wheel, gcc 13.3.
  - Elephant stayed resident on the card and idle throughout.

**What landed**
- `psionic/lib/kernel-search.coax` (`#authority`): the evaluator's decisions.
  - The declared workload: Elephant's offset RMS norm, 86 × 4,096, epsilon 1e-6.
  - Nine held-out cases: a decode row, widths 5,120, 1,000, 4,097, 1 and 17, f32 extremes, zero rows, and one huge value among tiny ones.
  - The contract: each output is one of the two f32 values next to the f64 norm, and an output still at the f32-maximum prefill counts as unwritten.
  - Timing statistics, the rule that "faster" means trials that don't overlap, and the tuner's choice.
- `psionic/lib/device/norm-search.coax` (`#authority`):
  - the four-kernel interface (knobs, plan, pass 0, pass 1);
  - the tuner's family `nt`: 49 knobs over chunks 1–128, contiguous or strided columns, 1, 2 or 4 outputs an item, and fused rows;
  - chapter 44's `dl_squares`/`dl_scale` behind the same interface, as `n30`.
- `psionic/tests/kernel_search.c`: the host. It runs each knob and case on the CPU (ascending and descending item order, each in its own process so a trap stops only that run), then on a CUDA device (same bits as the CPU, same bits on a repeat, the contract), and times 6 block sizes × 11 trials × 50 calls with CUDA events.
- `psionic/tests/kernel_search_eval.sh`: the only way in.
  - Steps: admission by `coax cuda`, the CPU gates, then a build and run on the L4 over IAP.
  - Each evaluation is written in full to `psionic/kernels/norm/ledger/NNN-PREFIX.log`, with the source's SHA-256, the time, the commit and, from evaluation 11 on, a digest of the evaluator.
  - The 8 agent evaluations are budgeted, failures included. `KS_CONFIRM=1` re-measures outside the budget and says so in the entry.
- `psionic/kernels/norm/agent/a1.coax` to `a8.coax`: the agent's candidates, unchanged since evaluation (their digests match the ledger).
- `psionic/tests/kernel_search_fixture.py`: renders the ledger as `psionic/fixtures/kernel-search-l4.coax`. Its `--check` mode confirms the file.
- `psionic/examples/kernel-search.coax` and `psionic/fixtures/kernel-search.out`.
- `psionic/tests/kernel_search_oracle.py`: an independent rebuild of the example using only the standard library. It regenerates the cases, reruns the three kernels' arithmetic in binary64 with one rounding, applies the contract, and re-reads the raw ledger.

**Process, as it happened**
- **Evaluations 1 and 2:** chapter 44 and the tuner, measured at 124.60 and 72.32 µs, on an earlier `norm-search.coax`.
- **Mutation fixes to `norm-search.coax`:**
  - the knob and plan kernels now write only from item 0;
  - stronger tests for partial sums and the test data;
  - a rewrite of the contiguous pass 0 with a bounds test per column. Evaluation 12 measured the tuner at 75.45 µs, with pass 0 at 21.16 µs instead of 18.00. Comparing the two sessions found that regression. The clamped loop was restored, and evaluation 14 measured 72.29 µs. The loop's clamp test (`hi>width` against `hi>=width`) is the equivalent mutant: both leave `hi` at `width`.
- **The agent:** a Claude subagent, in two sessions (115,374 and 174,274 tokens; 192 s and 838 s).
  - **Writable scope:** `psionic/kernels/norm/agent/` only. It was told not to read the evaluator, the host or the ledger; nothing enforced that. Afterwards no other file in the checkout had changed, and the evaluated sources match their digests.
  - **Session 1:** the evaluator's budget count used `grep | wc` under `pipefail`. With no agent entries yet, `grep` exited 1 and the script stopped silently. Four calls produced nothing and wrote no ledger entry. Fixed with `{ grep … || true; }`.
  - **Session 2:** spent the full budget of 8.
- **Evaluator time:** 415 s over 15 evaluations, 122 s of it the agent's 8. At the spot rate of about $0.42 an hour, the L4 time cost about five cents.
- **Compiler gap found:** a constant array used in a kernel (`for c:NT_CHUNKS`) compiles with `coax cuda` but references an undefined `coax_k0` under NVRTC. The tuner's knob list was rewritten as loops. The compiler was not changed.

**Not run:** the full `check.sh`, at the owner's request. `kernel-search` and `device/norm-search` are added to its Coax units. The VM `psionic-l4-elephant` was left running, as it was before this chapter.
