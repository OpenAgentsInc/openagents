## What the end-to-end runs proved

Both from the outside (the buyer's own BIP-340 and NIP-44 code) and from
the Pylon's log:

- The Pylon answered our relay's NIP-42 challenge (kind 22242 signed by the
  double, checked by the relay's `nip42_admit`), so its writes were
  accepted; every event it published (12 in the toy run, 9 in the Elephant
  run) got `OK true` after the relay's full id and signature check.
- The NIP-65 list names `wss://relay.openagents.com` and the local relay.
  The `30200` beacon verifies, says online v1, carries the content's
  SHA-256 as `x`, names `<pubkey>:pylon/decision` on lane `cj-decision`,
  and names the identity the engine reported at start.
- r-1: a 25910 encrypted by the buyer with `nostr-effect`'s NIP-44 was
  verified, decrypted by `nip44.coax`, admitted, decided and answered with
  a 26910 the buyer decrypted: `outcome answered`, `door pylon:ch19-local`,
  `provider` the Pylon's key, `identity` equal to the beacon's.
- r-2 (`v0`): 27010 error `unsupported_version`, no 26910.
- r-3/r-4 and a cancel for r-4 (toy engine): r-3 answered, r-4 dropped
  while waiting and answered 27010 `cancelled`, never a 26910.
- r-5/r-6 and the stop file: the beacon went online → draining → offline;
  both jobs answered after the stop; the offline beacon verifies and offers
  no free slot; the Pylon exited 0 after `OK` for the offline beacon.
- No forged input, no duplicates, no relay refusals.

**Elephant run.** The model was `elephant-1` on the Google Cloud L4 spot VM
(`psionic-l4-elephant`, [record](psionic-l4-elephant-2026-10-29.md)), reached
through the coordinator's IAP tunnel at `127.0.0.1:18797`; the VM was not
started, stopped or reconfigured. Identity
`elephant-1@sha256:682f02348dfe4617dd21bba2915c12e805de8cf16327a61e895a831ea681d0ba`
in the beacon and in every answer. r-1: `yes`, p 0.757202773 (chapter 10's
sample), engine time 326 ms; the start probe took 361 ms; buyer publish to
decrypted answer about 3.2 s, mostly three BIP-340 verifications under ASan
(relay twice, Pylon once). The toy run's r-1 took 4.8 s because of the 2.5 s
fixture delay.

## What is TEST-ONLY, and the exact swaps

Main at the start had none of openagents-d7's pieces; during the run
`coax/lib/crypto/schnorr_sign.coax` landed (`0cc86015e7`, 3,384/3,384
mutants killed). `event_sign.coax`, `message.coax` and NIP-44's two-key
conversation key had not landed when this was pushed.

1. **Signing** (`event_sign.coax`). In `run.coax`: add
   `#load "../../../coax/lib/nostr/event_sign.coax";`, change `PySignWork`
   to `NostrSignWork` in `Py.signer` (`signer:[1]NostrSignWork;`), and
   replace both calls `py_sign_step(` (in `py_publish` and `py_auth`) with
   `nostr_event_sign_step(` — same arguments, same `while n==0` loop. Then
   delete `py_sign_step`, `py_double`'s sign use, and `py_public` (take the
   pubkey from the first signed event, or the key's x-only point from
   `schnorr_sign.coax`), and stop spawning the double when nothing else
   uses it.
2. **NIP-44 conversation key from two keys.** In `py_seal` and
   `py_open_payload`, replace `py_shared_x(...)` followed by
   `nip44_conversation_key_checked(x, ck, false)` with the two-key entry
   (key, peer hex) → `ck`. Then delete `py_shared_x` and
   `psionic/tests/pylon/sign_double.ts`, and drop `--signer-bun`,
   `--signer-script`, `--node-path` from `py_config` and the harness.
3. **The key.** Once 1 and 2 are in, `--key-file` reads the `0600` file
   (a host effect that refuses group/other permissions) into `Py.key`;
   remove the refusal in `py_config`. `--test-key-label` stays for tests.
4. **Frames** (`message.coax`). In `py_take`, replace `py_frame(text)` with
   `nostr_message_read(text)` and map its type numbers (`NM_EVENT`=1 …
   `NM_NOTICE`=5, AUTH reported by verb) onto the existing branches; the
   REQ, CLOSE and EVENT writers in `py_subscribe`, `py_main` and
   `py_publish` become `nostr_message_write_req`/`_close`/`_event`. Delete
   `py_frame` and `PyFrame`.

`queue.coax` and the protocol units do not change for any of these.

## What is missing (not claimed)

- Reconnect and catch-up: the connection is opened once and a drop stops
  the Pylon. `coax/lib/nostr/relay.coax`'s machine is the place; it moves in
  with `message.coax`.
- `wss://`: `ws://` only; `tls-native.c` is not wired into this host.
- One decision at a time over HTTP; a cancel for the running job is logged,
  not acted on. No `27010 processing` progress events.
- The execution receipt carries `attempt_id`, `schema` and `transport`, not
  the `request_digest` the deployed reader checks.
- The owner's NIP-OA `auth` tag on the beacon; idempotency storage across
  restarts; NIP-11 discovery of the relay's limits.
- `run.coax` has no `#test`s of its own: it is glue, and the end-to-end run
  is its check. Its decisions are in the mutation-gated units.
- No published event has left 127.0.0.1. Nothing was published to
  `relay.openagents.com` or any public relay.
