# Chapter 36: many questions at once, 2026-10-30

## What changed

- `psionic/lib/device/plan.coax` (`#authority`): `dp_plan_batch`. One suffix
  pass for up to 8 questions that share a kept prefix: token-wise launches over
  all their tokens, while the convolution, delta rule, value projection and
  attention, and the readout, run per question on that question's rows.
  `dp_batch_admits` is the admission rule: same prefix start, at most 512
  tokens, and one projection path for every question (all at least 16 tokens,
  or all together fewer than 16). Metal is refused. Also `dp_batch_layout`,
  `dp_batch_logits`, `dp_batch_workspace`, and the checker `dp_state_digest`.
  The existing plans are not touched.
- `psionic/platform/elephant-gpu.c`/`.h`: `eg_decide_batch`. It runs the
  prefix pass unless the fork buffer holds the same prefix tokens, stages
  each question at its rows, runs one batched pass and downloads each
  question's logits. A batch the plan refuses runs one `eg_decide_fork` at a
  time.
- `psionic/tests/lab_request.h`, included by `elephant_device.c` and
  `mammoth_device.c`: the Lab request whole, forked, batched, and batched with
  the prefix kept from the last request. It fails unless batched and forked
  logits are bit-identical. Both harnesses now place for 256 device tokens.
- The example `psionic/examples/many-questions.coax` with its fixture
  `psionic/fixtures/many-questions.out`, the oracle
  `psionic/tests/batch_oracle.py`, the chapter
  `psionic/book/ch036-many-questions-at-once.md`, and the README, SUMMARY and
  PLAN rows.

## Correctness bar, stated before the device runs

The bar was the same for both models. Each question's batched logits must be
bit-identical to its sequential forked suffix (0 of 248,320 differ), and the
whole decision must pass the frozen 0.0005 rule against the recorded CPU
result and the independent reference. No reduction-order profile was needed:
the admission rule keeps every question's projection path. Existing plan
digests must not change for unbatched requests.

## Device runs

The host was `coderos-4080`: NixOS, RTX 4080, coax0 sources generated on the
Mac at this commit and compiled there with gcc 15 `-std=gnu17`. The original
Elephant (`elephant-1-b3f8a934`) and Mammoth (`mammoth-1-61cec44c`) weights
were authenticated by their sessions. The GPU switch reported `stopped` and
the card was free (386 MiB in use) before each run. Under the owner's change
of 2026-10-30, gpt-oss was not restarted afterwards, and the card was left
free.

## Limits

- The chapter 44 Elephant placement now streams 7 feed-forward matrices
  (0.66 GiB), because its workspace is sized for 256 tokens. The "resident"
  Elephant column is that placement, and both of its before/after numbers
  use it.
- The `/v1/systemone` server (`serve-host.c`) still decides its questions
  one at a time, so its Lab latency is unchanged.
- The Metal plan refuses batches.
- Prefix reuse across requests is the engine's single kept prefix, keyed by
  the placed model and the exact prefix tokens. Any new prefix evicts it, and
  a multi-entry cache is not built.
- Mixed short and long suffixes fall back to sequential forks.
- This was one machine and one request shape, with unpinned clocks on a
  shared host.
