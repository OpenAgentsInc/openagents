## Failures and boundaries

- The initial six arithmetic questions supply no improvement headroom.
  Baseline harder development is2/6, four incomplete limits with no detected
  triple repetition. This is not a demonstrated original-model loop.
- Scorer-v1 rejects valid H₂O. The raw response and historical boolean stay;
  scorer-v2 handles all subscript digits. Reported comparisons use v2.
- All three text-only subjects return `[1,1,0]`: no changed proposal and no
  repair credit. Their same-digest carries are zero new sequences.
- The selected changed diagnostic candidate is worse on development,1/6
  versus2/6. The held-out worksheet never reselects a candidate.
- Zeroing the final row is deliberately destructive. Incomplete parser
  status6 is separate from malformed status4 and from actual repeated spans.
- Setup compilation initially used unsupported foreign structs/views and
  process capture truncated giant generated C lines at its per-line limit.
  Primitive-view effect signatures and fixed-argv raw output redirection
  correct those setup failures; they generated no scientific trials.
- Initial window mutation has2 survivors (count16384 and dtype boundary);
  isolated boundary checks remove masking by a second invalid dimension or
  wrong byte length. Initial proposal mutation has4 survivors (array guard
  and sentinel changes); a three-key object check and adjacent out-of-range
  sentinels expose them. No equivalent-mutant exemptions were used.
- The protocol's first-channel confidence wording exceeds the captured
  measurement. Actual reports use first-answer-token NLL/confidence after
  forced-final framing. Full-sequence loss/channel-confidence remains unrun.

The fit is one frozen-direction scalar AdamW channel objective, not full
LoRA/SFT. Random edits are exact count/norm-matched constrained permutations,
not isotropic directions. Three sampler seeds do not create independent
baseline/model blocks. Retained tasks are a small finite worksheet; they do
not establish general capability preservation. Rank-one recipes may become
rank two after per-coefficient BF16 rounding.

No Qwen3.5 generation support, full LoRA comparison, interior QK/OV edit,
reproduced loop repair, broad model-family study, independent statistical
superiority or live shadow activation is claimed. The new runnable CPU
window profile and recorded original Metal experiment are the delivered
scope; `qualified_targets` does not promote an experimental patch mechanism
to a general Metal library API.
