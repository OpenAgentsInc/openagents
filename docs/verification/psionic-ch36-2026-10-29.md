The `gpt-oss-attention-cpu-wide-v1` profile takes projected f32 Q/K/V and
per-query-head sink logits, with 64-wide heads and divisible query/KV head
counts through 64/8. It rotates Q/K with split-half YaRN, keeps only the
sliding window's KV entries, and includes the unscaled sink only in the softmax
denominator. Full causal capacity is bounded to 128; sliding capacity equals
the 1–128 window and admits absolute positions 0–131,071. Current entries and
whole context publish together, preserving public state on refusal.

`gptoss-math.coax` also supplies a staged bounded biased projection and
interleaved clamped SwiGLU, with exact changed-lane counts. Shared projection
limits include the 1,048,576-MAC budget. The arithmetic profile uses f64 rotary
math and compensated products/reductions, f64 scores/exponentials and f32 stored
coefficients, rotated vectors and output. FP contraction and fast math are off.

gate checks the oracle fixtures for drift and compiles native references
without upstream dependencies. `psionic/check.sh` includes both new units, the
oracle and the native harness; the entire earlier multi-chapter mutation suite
was not rerun for this addition.
