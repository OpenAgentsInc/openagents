## Original qualification

The [policy](../../psionic/tests/v2-qualification-policy.json) was frozen before
the original candidate run: logits/residuals require absolute error at most
`0.0002 + 0.00002 * abs(reference)`; route weights use
`0.000002 + 0.00002 * abs(reference)`; ordered router IDs and every greedy
decision must match exactly, with EOS required. Limits are failures here.

Each cache form executes eight prompt tokens followed by the chosen
continuation: 31 complete model steps and 24 generated IDs including EOS.
The independent NumPy oracle evaluates the complete causal prefix without
incremental caching, reading original BF16 directly and applying the declared
F32 boundaries with binary64 products/reductions. It imports no upstream
model implementation. Its input prefix is the candidate's prompt plus emitted
IDs before EOS; exact next-token comparison at every step proves the complete
autoregressive path, since the first different choice would fail.

Both forms produce the same bytes, including the leading space:
