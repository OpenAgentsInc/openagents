The original converter explicitly interprets low nibble first at even K.
The raw header identifies routed weights as I8-packed FP4 E2M1 with separate
UE8M0 scales per row and 32 K elements. The shared FFN is FP8 E4M3 with scales
per 32 × 32 block. The plan and target notes were corrected to these
source/header facts. Existing gpt-oss APIs remain separate.

Every finite FP4 and FP8 code/UE8M0 pair decodes exactly to binary64, including
signed zero and values above F32's range. Scale 255 refuses; FP8 codes 127/255
also refuse. The public decoder stages the whole result. Encoded projection
reads coefficients directly, rounds activations onto the original per-32 FP8
grid and uses checked F32 projection output boundaries. SiLU/up rounds to F32,
then route multiplication rounds to F32 before the down-input quantization.
Up clamps both sides at 10; gate clamps only from above.
