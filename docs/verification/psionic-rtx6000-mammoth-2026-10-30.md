## Blackwell (compute 12.0)

Nothing in the repository changed. `cuda-host.c` compiles for
`compute_<major><minor>`, so NVRTC built all 21 kernels for `compute_120`
(3.57 s the first time, 0.05 s after), and the `mma.sync.m16n8k16` BF16
instruction the warp kernels use runs on sm_120. The 4080's results cannot
have moved: no source, plan or kernel changed.

`psionic/tests/run_device_warp.sh` (chapter 37's check of the MMA primitive
against its Coax meaning and of the projections against the CPU profile)
passes on the card:

| --- | --- | --- |

The Blackwell tensor cores round some f32 partial sums differently from the
Ada ones, so whole decisions differ from the 4080's in the sixth or seventh
decimal (Elephant p(yes) 0.757197863 here, 0.757202773 on the 4080 and the
L4). Both are inside the frozen rule; on this card both models land closer to
the CPU result than on the 4080.

## Acceptance test, frozen before the first device run

Chapter 46's rule, committed in `run_mammoth_device.sh` before this card was
used: the choice must be chapter 15's (`yes`) and each of the four statistics
within 0.0005 of chapter 15's recorded CPU result (`--check-cpu`); the
harness also checks chapter 15's independent layer-streamed reference
(`--check-output`). The CPU decision was not rerun. All three Mammoth runs
(first, restart, 13 GiB budget) printed the same values and passed both
checks.

Largest difference from the CPU result: 1.40e-5 (slot mass; 2.15e-5 on the

| Process start to placed engine | 325.4 s | 243.5 s | 255.2 s |

The cold open is the checkpoint hash on one CPU core: 171 MB/s reading from
the boot disk, 227 MB/s from the page cache. The upload itself is under two
seconds. The Mammoth harness has no verified-file record (chapter 19), so a
restart hashes again; with one, a restart would be a few seconds.

## Warm latency

Chapter 14's sample (`Water is wet.` / `Is the water wet?`, 86 tokens,
49-token prefix), time from the host's request to the probabilities:

| Measure | RTX PRO 6000, resident | RTX PRO 6000, 13.09 GiB budget | RTX 4080, 13.09 GiB budget (ch30b) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single decision | 100 runs: P50 265.2, P95 265.4, min 256.1, max 266.8, mean 262.8 ms; restart, 100 runs: P50 265.5, P95 265.7, min 256.4, max 265.8 ms | 10 runs: 668.2-668.9 ms | 3 runs: 1601.4-1625.1 ms |
| Forked: prefix pass + suffix / suffix alone | 442.5 / 218.7 ms | 1289.5 / 666.8 ms | 3091.7 / 1599.3 ms |
| Lab request, 5 whole decisions | 1285.1, 1285.4 ms; restart 1284.4, 1272.2 ms | 3342.7, 3341.9 ms | 8009.5, 8001.6 ms |
| Lab request, forked | 1326.6, 1326.4 ms; restart 1311.4, 1311.3 ms | 3961.7, 3965.6 ms | 9494.2, 9491.8 ms |

The single-decision times are bimodal (27 runs at 256.1-256.4 ms, 73 at
263.8-266.8 ms); clocks were not pinned. During the decisions the card ran at
2,287 MHz, 100% utilization, about 304 W and 42 C.

Fully resident, Mammoth answers in 265 ms, 6.0x the 4080's streamed 1.6 s,
and the Lab request takes 1.29 s against 8.0 s. The fork still does not pay
for this request shape: a 37-token suffix costs 219 ms of a 265 ms decision,
so five forked questions (one prefix pass plus five suffixes) take slightly
longer than five whole decisions.

The 13.09 GiB run reproduces the 4080's placement on this host (12.53 GiB
resident, 35.20 GiB streamed through two 170 MiB slots). It takes 668 ms, so
the link carries 37.8 GB at about 56.6 GB/s, against 23.6 GB/s on the 4080's
PCIe 4.0 host: the same plan streams 2.4 times faster here.

## Machine time and cost

Created 05:57:37 UTC and deleted 06:30:56 UTC (00:57-01:31 CDT): 33 minutes,
0.56 hours, with no preemption. At the spot rate of about $1.40-1.60 an hour
the machine cost about $0.80-0.90; the 200 GB disk for the same half hour
adds about a cent. The VM, its boot disk and both firewall rules are deleted;
`gcloud compute instances list` and `gcloud compute disks list` show nothing
named `psionic-rtx6000*`.

## Limits

- One spot VM, one driver, one request shape; clocks not pinned.
- The Coax outputs were generated on an Apple host, not by a compiler built on
  the VM.
- Per-kernel qualification was run for Elephant on this card, not for Mammoth
  (the Mammoth harness has no qualify mode); Mammoth's launches differ from
  Elephant's only in counts.
- Chapter 170's load generator was not run: the server does not place Mammoth.
- Prompts longer than 128 tokens were not compared against the CPU reference.
