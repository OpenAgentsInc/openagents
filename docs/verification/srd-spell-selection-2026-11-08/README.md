# Canonical spell selections and readiness

2026-11-08. Baseline `7f7d7302e5`. Partial progress on
[#13038](https://github.com/OpenAgentsInc/openagents/issues/13038) and preset
state needed by [#13044](https://github.com/OpenAgentsInc/openagents/issues/13044).
The containing commit and [source hashes](source-hashes.txt) identify this work.

## Behavior

Class membership alone no longer permits casting. Shared ordinary, Quickened,
Shield and Hellish Rebuke admission require a selected source spell or an
explicit always-prepared/feature/stat-block/world grant. Helpers use their
parent's selection. Unprepared refusals preserve resources, dice, events and
turn budgets; selected reactions still spend normally. Everglade's explicit
house/bar grants remain creature-local.

`spell-selection.coax` owns cantrip/prepared limits, class/level eligibility,
Wizard spellbook membership, unique counts and atomic loadout replacement.
Invalid indices, foreign/too-high choices, overfull selections and missing
Wizard knowledge refuse. Failed replacement preserves every selected/known
word and count. Empty replacement clears every selected word. Repeated choices
are idempotent. Known leveled spells and selected cantrips/prepared spells are
separate state.

Generated `data/spell-index.coax` retains all 339 textual source IDs, levels and
independent class-table masks, 78 executable ID mappings including helpers,
the source pin and named Wizard setup keys. Mage Armor and other unimplemented
spells can exist in a loadout/book without inventing executable IDs. Source
indices are snapshot-local; persist textual IDs. The runtime projection contains
no effect programs or descriptions; the original six-file PDF importer is unchanged.
Its small executable-to-parent-ID registry is checked against every actual
record. The builder loads the registry instead of importing the engine or its
own generated output, avoiding a regeneration cycle when new IDs are assigned.

`spell-loadouts.coax` declares preset priorities and submits ordinary choices
through the authority. All eight caster presets through level 6 have exactly
their base class-table cantrip/prepared counts. Always-prepared Circle, Life,
Devotion, Smite and Favored Enemy grants stay separate from ordinary selections.
Wizard presets know six starting spells plus two class research choices per
level, with preparation reserved for precast Mage Armor. Unimplemented source
choices fill remaining capacity without becoming castable. Existing high-level
Warlock Arcanum choices remain explicit grants; their full high-level resource
rules are not qualified here.

Experimental filters its bar by the same readiness. Its 12-class, levels 1–6
regression verifies every displayed spell is selected or granted, including
combined Produce Flame. Ability identities, ordering and sound bindings remain
stable. Spell-mechanics fixtures now configure legal alternate selections where
their scenario needs one. The parked Polymorph witness explicitly prepares its
spell and provides a level-7 caster/slot, so its failure still diagnoses missing
transformation instead of being hidden by preparation.

## Remaining work

These APIs configure owned character state; they do not perform Long Rests,
level-ups, paid copying or runtime choice UI. Full feature/origin grant
provenance (including unimplemented granted spells), class/subclass behavior and
legal progression remain active work. Components/focus/cost/consumption,
required casting workflows and explicit upcast/multiple-target choices still
keep #13038 open. Capacity failures remain active and Polymorph stays parked.
This change does not qualify every selected spell's mechanics or close an issue.
