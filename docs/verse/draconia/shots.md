## How D1 is made

1. **Stills.**
   - `ffmpeg` pulls one still per shot (the middle frame) and a 4 fps strip
     per shot into `~/work/film-out/draconia/ref/`.
   - They're the target for framing, lens and light.
2. **Shot files.**
   - One `docs/verse/film/verse-shots/draconia-NN.json` per shot, holding:
     - the scene;
     - the rig keys (eye, target, focal mm, ease, plus the new `fstop` and
       `focus` keys);
     - shake and subframes.
   - Shots render through `crates/verse/examples/film_shot.rs` with a new
     scene kind `courtyard` (the D1 set).
   - The aspect is 21:9.
3. **Side-by-side check.** For each shot, the reference and the Verse render
   are stacked or put side by side (`ffmpeg hstack`), into
   `~/Downloads/draconia-d1-side-by-side.mp4`, at 1080p for the owner's
   review.
4. **The cut.** `scripts/film/cut.py` assembles shots 1–6 at the reference's
   cut times, with the reference audio for the internal study only.
5. **Fidelity rules:**
   - match the reference's framing, lens, light direction, colour temperature
     and motion;
   - keep Verse's realistic look (PBR, real skin and fabric), not toon
     shading;
   - where we can't match something yet, write it down in the shot's
     `notes`.
