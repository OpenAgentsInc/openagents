# Everglade design docs

Specifications for Everglade systems that aren't yet chapters of
[Building Everglade](../book/README.md).

- [Agentics: a profession for commanding agents](agentics-profession-spec.md):
  the binder's profession, the Binding Table in your house, agents that work
  in the world, and the bridge that lets them act on your OpenAgents account.
- [Housewright: gathering materials and building houses](housewright-profession-spec.md):
  gather branches, withies, reeds, clay and stone, make primitive tools, and
  build from a branch lean-to to a stone cottage on a plot in the city
  clearing, with a four-branch talent tree.
