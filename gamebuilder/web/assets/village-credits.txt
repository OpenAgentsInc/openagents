gamebuilder.org: the medieval village's art

The buildings and rocks of the medieval village are from 0 A.D., a free
software game. 0 A.D. art by Wildfire Games (https://www.wildfiregames.com/),
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
licence (CC BY-SA 3.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The models (0 A.D.'s names):
  structures/britons/house
  structures/gauls/house
  structures/gauls/market
  geology/highland1_moss
  geology/highland2
with their base-colour textures (brit_struct_1, celt_struct_1, celt_prop_1,
celt_props_new, wrld_rock_highlands, wrld_rock_highlands_moss).

What was changed. Each model was exported from 0 A.D.'s files to glTF with
its transforms baked, moved so the middle of its footprint is over the origin
(its own ground level kept), and written as one vertex and index
buffer in an OpenAgents Metaverse Engine asset pack (VPK1). Its base-colour
textures were resized to 512 by 512 pixels and re-encoded as JPEG (quality
92); its normal and specular maps were left out. No triangle was removed. On
the page the models are scaled (1.3 to 2.6 times), turned in quarter turns
and lit by the page's own renderer.

The changed art is offered under the same licence, CC BY-SA 3.0. It is these
files, which anyone may fetch:
  https://gamebuilder.org/assets/village.pack        the pack (models and textures)
  https://gamebuilder.org/assets/village-pack.coax   the tool that built it, with the pack's layout
  https://gamebuilder.org/assets/village-credits.txt this note

The rest of gamebuilder.org (its code, its other art) is not part of this
art and is not under this licence.
