Credits

Assets, typefaces, and engine software used by the worlds hosted on gamebuilder.org.

Fated Dragon

World design and stone megalith architecture by Kiki David. Uses the OpenAgents Shapechange dragon model and animations (CC0 1.0 Public Domain).

SPLICEBOTS

Ported from Unreal Engine 5.9 into Coax on WebGPU. Uses IBM Plex Mono and Michroma typefaces (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and includes the Microduck mesh notice from the SPLICEBOTS source project.

The Whispering Spring

World, grove and quail by Kiki David. The earthen bank, cedars, limestone and roots come from the Obsidian Quail grove asset kit; everything else is procedural geometry written in Coax. Every sound is synthesized in the browser; the album's recordings, lore and the quail's words are placeholders until the artist supplies them, and will be credited here.

Ice Cream Man

Original seeded escape game compiled directly from Coax (metaverse/icecream/) with procedural house geometry, ink-outlined toon shading, and vector HUD strokes.

Scrappers Arena

GameBuilder's original team arena shooter (gamebuilder/core/play.coax and modes.coax) with Blender-crafted Bolt, Boulder, and Lumen character models (scrappers.gbm), stylized WebGPU lighting, and synthesized WebAudio sound effects.

Typography

Geist and Geist Mono by Vercel, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

OpenAgents Metaverse Engine & Coax

All worlds compile from Coax to WebAssembly and WGSL shaders running on WebGPU. Built on OpenAgents.
