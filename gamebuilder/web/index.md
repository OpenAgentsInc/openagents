# GAMEBUILDER

Playable 3D worlds in the browser — built in Coax on the OpenAgents Metaverse Engine with WebGPU. No downloads and no separate login (accounts live at [openagents.com](https://openagents.com)).

## Worlds

1. [Fated Dragon](https://gamebuilder.org/fated-dragon): Take wing as a fire-breathing dragon over a 2.2 km stone megalith — three levels of marble galleries, 200-step sky atriums, Victorian gardens, cobbled avenues, and eight sleeping dragons on the summit plaza.
2. [SPLICEBOTS](https://gamebuilder.org/splicebots): Ported from Unreal Engine 5.9 to Coax on WebGPU — explore the solar farm and desert compound, scavenge scrap and batteries, craft Microduck companions, and battle raiders through the night garage.
3. [The Whispering Spring](https://gamebuilder.org/whispering-spring): Obsidian Quail's album as a sacred grove at dusk — follow the quail, touch the spring, stack creek stones and hang a prayer ribbon beside everyone else's; each ritual opens a page of her story.
4. [Ice Cream Man](https://gamebuilder.org/ice-cream-man): Stuck in a seeded Airbnb house — find three check-out keys, freeze the Ice Cream Man with Hot Fudge or push him back with the Fart Launcher, hide in closets, and unlock the front door to escape.
5. [Scrappers Arena](https://gamebuilder.org/scrappers-arena): Team arena shooter with Bolt, Boulder, and Lumen — play 1v1 to 5v5 across Knockouts, Last Stand, Scrapball, and Hoops in first or third person with class Supers.
