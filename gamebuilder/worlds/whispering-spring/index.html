        The grove, in words

        Cedars lean over a small pool at the foot of a tall, dark cut bank. Roots hang out of the earth above the water. On the right the pool spills over a low lip of pale limestone into a shallow brook. To the left lies a scatter of creek stones beside a flat slab, and a cedar holds one bare branch out over the pool. A black quail with a curled topknot goes about its morning in front of you.
