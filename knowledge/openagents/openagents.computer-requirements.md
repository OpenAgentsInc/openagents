## Answer

To run Coder you need your own Mac or Linux computer on the same tailnet as your phone, running the Coder host. We don't provide a hosted computer yet. Without one, you can still chat with us in the Chat tab.

## Details

- Cloud in the chat's selector is the basic chat with us, not a computer.
- Chatting needs no computer.
