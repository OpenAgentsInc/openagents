## Answer

Right now you earn trainer XP with our six tutorial quests: rerun one of Microcoder's published Terminal-Bench passes from its pinned recipe on your own machine, pass the task's tests, and publish a reproduction signed with your trainer key. Our referee checks it and awards 50 XP; the six together are worth 300 XP, level 3. XP is a record, not money.

## Details

- You need Docker, Microcoder, a Codex login, and a Jev key; a tutorial run costs about a cent at list price.
- The season `tb21-tutorial-s1` closes 2026-12-25.
- Each quest pays once per reproducer.
