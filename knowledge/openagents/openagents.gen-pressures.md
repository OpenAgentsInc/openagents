## Answer

The essay answers each pressure by putting specialization in members and discipline in the rule for bringing members in. Execution: the front delegates work that needs a computer to Coder, and reliability is how the composition checks results. Local knowledge: supplied as members and context. Evaluation: capability claims, reproduced and validated. Cost: spend judgment before thinking. A buyer: members presented as jobs. Authority: describing an operation grants nothing, and a grant is a separate record.

## Details

- The 0.99 to the 100th problem is not solved; it is moved to a place where each step is checked. Coder works a GitHub issue in its own worktree, runs the repository's checks, and pushes only when they pass.
- On evaluation, Project map passed 5 of 6 tests with the plugin against 2 of 6 without, three trainer keys reproduced it, a second test set validated it at 4 of 6 against 2 of 6, and it was adopted into coder-defaults. The validated delta was smaller than the original, which is the finding the method exists to surface.
- On cost, the router's judgment takes about 170 ms at the median, a prepared answer reaches the phone in 0.6 to 0.7 s, and a full model answer takes 3 to 5 s.
- On authority, the computer's owner sets which engines may run; a paired phone can ask for one but cannot widen the owner's policy. Generality of the front does not imply generality of permission.
- The market drafts, NIP-MKT and NIP-LAB, are partial drafts, not a deployed market. None of the answers is free and none is complete.
