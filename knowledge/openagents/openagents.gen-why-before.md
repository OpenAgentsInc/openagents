## Answer

A facilitator routing requests to specialist agents over a common language existed in the 1990s. Four things were missing, and each arrived only recently: a front that can read any request and give calibrated choices code can act on; executors worth delegating to, since coding agents act on real environments; a way to know a member helps, which is the claim-and-adoption discipline; and an open, signed substrate for members and evidence, so a member is not subject to one vendor's review, terms, and revenue share.

## Details

- Without measuring whether adding an agent made the system better, more members means more ways to fail.
- Signed events on Nostr let anyone publish a plugin, a test, or a result, and let anyone verify who signed it.
