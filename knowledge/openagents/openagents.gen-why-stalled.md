## Answer

The 2023 boom put a broadly capable model in a generic loop and expected a dependable worker; by late 2024 the industry had turned to vertical agents. The essay reads that turn as six pressures: planning is not doing, real work needs local knowledge, narrow work can be evaluated, open-ended execution has unpredictable cost, a defined job has a buyer, and authority must be bounded. The industry did not abandon generality; it learned what it takes to turn it into dependable work. The open question was where the specialization should live.

## Details

- A hundred steps at 99 % each succeed about 37 % of the time; GAIA's original agents passed 15 % where people passed 92 %.
- Coding was not just one vertical: code is a general way to act on digital information, so a narrow start can be a road to generality.
- General agents never went away; they became more engineered, with Magentic-One, Agent Skills, MCP, and Agent2Agent supplying pieces of a composition. What none supplied was a way to know that adding a piece made the agent better.
