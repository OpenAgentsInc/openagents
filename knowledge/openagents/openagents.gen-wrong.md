## Answer

A thesis that cannot fail is not one. Five results would count against ours: adopted plugins stop helping on the work people bring; typed routing cannot keep its precision as members grow; independent contributors do not arrive or verify, so the open in the argument is decorative; vertical products keep winning on the same jobs by a margin members cannot close; or evidence costs so much that good members never reach adoption. We intend to publish the measurements that would show each, whichever way they come out.
