## Answer

We're bringing the Gym into this chat. Soon you'll be able to ask what's new, test a tool such as Project map on Coder with and without it, make a tool and its tests with us, add a result to the Gym, check other trainers' results, and see the XP your work earns. Until the first results are published, the Gym's news here comes from our changelog and notes.

## Details

- Published results, checks, and adoptions appear in the Gym's news with their source once they exist.
- The Gym in the Verse keeps its boards for reviewing results.
