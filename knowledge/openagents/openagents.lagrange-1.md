## Answer

Lagrange 1 is a construction station zone in Verse, orbiting the Sun-Earth L1 point, with real orbital mechanics. In the OpenAgents app's Grid its portal is hidden for now, in every build, so you can't enter it from the Grid yet.

## Details

- The portal's site stays in the Grid; the gate constant keeps it closed.
