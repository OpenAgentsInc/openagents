## Answer

You can stop testing at any time. We delete session notes and recordings on request. Signed events on relays, like XP awards and playtest records, can't be deleted, but an award can be revoked, and a revocation is public. You can delete the local playtest log in Account > Playtest.

## Details

- You choose which key earns XP, and it doesn't have to be tied to your name.
