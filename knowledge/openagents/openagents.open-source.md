## Answer

We're open source. The app, the chat worker, Coder, and our protocols are in github.com/OpenAgentsInc/openagents under the Apache License 2.0, so you can read, build, and talk about all of it.

## Details

- The chat worker is `crates/coder/src/bin/coder-worker.rs`; the app's Rust library is `crates/openagents-mobile`.
- Account links to the source code.
