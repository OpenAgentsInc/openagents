## Answer

Playtest XP comes only from contributions a triager accepts: accepted feedback (10 XP), reproducible bug reports (20 XP, or 50 for the most severe), a design finding that shipped (30), confirming a fix (10), and completed session scripts or diary weeks with an accepted report. Joining, installing, or time in the app earns nothing. A separate playtest referee signs it, it shows beside trainer XP, and it never pays money.

## Details

- Uniqueness: the first accepted reporter per issue earns it.
- A full, active season comes to roughly 250 to 400 playtest XP.
