## Answer

Our playtest is open: anyone can join through the public TestFlight link on iPhone or the signed APK on Android. Season 1 runs from 2026-09-29 to 2026-10-26. Joining earns nothing by itself; XP and titles come only from contributions we accept, such as reproducible bug reports, and nothing pays money.

## Details

- We test the app, not you; there are no wrong answers.
- The repository is open source, so there's no confidentiality.
