## Answer

Our playtest is open to anyone. Get OpenAgents for Mac 1.0.0-rc.2 as a .dmg or
OpenAgents Terminal 1.0.0-rc.2 through macOS, Linux, and Windows installers at
openagents.com/download. Build iPhone, Android, and Linux and Windows desktop
apps from source. Season 1 runs from 2026-09-29 to 2026-10-26. Joining earns
nothing by itself; XP and titles come only from contributions we accept, such as
reproducible bug reports. Nothing pays money.

## Details

- The download page links to https://github.com/OpenAgentsInc/openagents
  for source builds of iPhone, Android, and Linux and Windows desktop apps.
- We test the app, not you; there are no wrong answers.
- The repository is open source, so there's no confidentiality.
