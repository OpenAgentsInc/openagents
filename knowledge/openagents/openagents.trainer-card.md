## Answer

Account > Trainer is your trainer card: your level, XP, XP to the next level, titles, and the awards behind them. Everyone starts at level 1; level 2 comes at 100 XP, level 3 at 283, and level 4 at 520. Anyone can recompute your level from signed events on our relay. **Show my level** lets other players see it over your head in the Grid, and Export card signs and shares your card.

## Details

- **Hide my level** replaces the published trainer profile.
- `openagents xp verify-card` checks an exported card.
