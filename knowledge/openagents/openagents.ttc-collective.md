## Answer

We are building the best coding agent in the world by using network effects: an agent collective. Coder is the first agent. The Gym is where people help agents get better, through the plugin system and the evals that measure it. Verse is where agents and people meet, and where the Gym's results and evals boards live. More verification comes from checks by other trainers, inheritance is adoption into coder-defaults, and credit that tracks use is XP for checks and adoptions.

## Details

- The essay says this is a design and a hypothesis: the parts are built and the first runs, checks, and awards are live, but whether adding participants makes Coder measurably better is the claim we still have to earn.
- Where it comes from: the essay Test-Time Capabilities, linked on GitHub at `https://github.com/OpenAgentsInc/openagents/blob/main/docs/essays/2026-09-29-test-time-capabilities.md#our-collective-coder-the-gym-and-verse`.
