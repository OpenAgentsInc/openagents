## Answer

Part Three lists our open problems. A validation by someone else: the first validation and adoption used one operator's keys. Revalidate after adoption: nothing yet reopens a claim when its key moves. State the distribution a suite stands for. Publish the effect, not only the verdict. Record the attempts, not only the winner. Run Jev-probe as a subject somewhere it can run, and pin what can be pinned in the baseline.

## Details

- On revalidation, the essay says that until a job exists, an adopted plugin keeps its place until its admission expires, which is a default nobody measured.
- A claim with cost as its primary outcome, which the router's cheaper tiers are, has no gate yet.
