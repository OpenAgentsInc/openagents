## Answer

Capability credit is recognition for the people whose work made a capability claim real, for the events that show the work was used, and for verification work whichever way it came out. The principle is to credit verification work, not agreement: a rerun that followed the protocol earns credit whether it confirms or disputes. A rule that pays only confirming reruns builds a quiet preference for agreement into the network. Adoption still requires positive evidence.

## Details

- Credit for activity such as runs, publishes, and downloads rewards components that exist; credit for independent reproduction and adoption rewards components that help.
- A competent evaluator who finds that a celebrated claim does not reproduce has contributed more information than the fourth person who confirms it.
- To measure it: credit records that anyone can recompute from the public rerun and adoption events, with disputes paid at the same rate as confirmations.
