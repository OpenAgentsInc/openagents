## Answer

Test-time delegation is acquiring another agent's capability for one task by handing it a prepared briefing, then recording what it did as part of the same task. The strongest capability for a task is sometimes another agent, and treating it as a capability means its contribution is measured, not assumed. Delegation is also where authority is allocated: let designation carry authority, so when the judgment selects the three files a delegate should read, the host grants those three, not the repository.

## Details

- Authority attenuates at each hand-off, from user to agent to delegate to plugin to resource; a child receives no more than its parent held except by an explicit new grant from a principal entitled to give one.
- To measure it: the same outcome, cost, and time as any attempt, compared with the attempt made without delegating.
