## Answer

Push the faint stick at the bottom left to walk. In touch look, push the stick at the bottom right, or drag anywhere, to look around; both thumbs work at once. Double-tap to jump and pinch to zoom. Between the sticks, one button switches touch look and motion look, where the camera follows the phone, and the crosshair button recenters the camera.

## Details

- Motion look hides the look stick.
- Pinching in past the nearest orbit switches to first person.
