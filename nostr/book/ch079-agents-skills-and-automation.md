# 79. Agents, skills and automation

> **Status: unimplemented — design draft.** This chapter is written ahead of
> its code. It has no source-linked listings or examples.

Agents are defined, published and scheduled. This chapter covers the four NIPs
that describe them.

Part X, Every NIP. Result: NIP-AGENT saved agents (30187), NIP-SKILL published
`SKILL.md` listings (30188), NIP-SOV sovereign agents with local custody, and
NIP-AUTO scheduled and triggered work.

## What this chapter teaches

- AGENT: name, instructions, runtime ID; private carriage and public agents.
- SKILL: kind 30188 listings reviewed by openagents.com.
- SOV: immutable profile, bounded activation, custody, signing and decryption.
- AUTO: immutable plans, occurrences, continuation and recovery.
