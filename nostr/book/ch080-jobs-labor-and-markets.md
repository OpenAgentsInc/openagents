# 80. Jobs, labor and markets

> **Status: unimplemented — design draft.** This chapter is written ahead of
> its code. It has no source-linked listings or examples.

OpenAgents sells work over Nostr. These NIPs replace NIP-90's general machine
with specific contracts.

Part X, Every NIP. Result: NIP-CJ encrypted conversation, typed-decision and
execution jobs, NIP-LAB agent labor terms and delivery, and NIP-MKT offerings,
negotiated orders and Bitcoin settlement.

## What this chapter teaches

- CJ: job families, encryption, typed decision jobs and conformance.
- LAB: labor terms, linking an order to execution, verification, review and
  disputes.
- MKT: offerings, negotiation, admission, completion and fixed-price Bitcoin
  settlement.
