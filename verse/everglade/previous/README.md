# The previous Everglade

What the Rust Verse engine's Everglade was, copied unchanged from the
commit before it was removed (`0b401561a0^`, the "Nuke" of October 27,
2026), as the reference for bringing today's Coax Everglade
(`docs/verse/everglade.md`, `verse/everglade/plan.coax`) to parity with it.
Links inside these files point at the old tree (`crates/verse*`,
`assets/verse/...`); read them with `git show 0b401561a0^:<path>`.

- `everglade-map.png`, `everglade-map.svg`: the illustrated map of
  Everglade grown into a city around its commons.
- `everglade.md`: the old Everglade: its kits and pack, budgets, lighting
  and sky, the player (the skinned male Ranger and its clips), the Agent
  Studio and the browser build.
- `everglade-medieval-refactor.md`, `everglade-medieval-handoff.md`: the
  medieval-town rebuild of Everglade and its handoff.
- `water.md`, `coast.md`: the water system and the coast, first built
  for Everglade's river and shore.
- `destructible-buildings.md`: Everglade's town breaking under the
  demolition rules.
- `generative-agents.md`: the city's people as generative agents.
